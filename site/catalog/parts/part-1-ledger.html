P1-C01 — Thursday warning

  • Coverage: P0001–P0139, independently read in full. Consulted all five existing fact catalogs and chapter review; corrected the inherited summary and C001/C002 labels.
  • People: Pavel Prishchepa, an engineer in narrator Andrei Semipalov's laboratory, introduces astrophysicist Alexei Gamov (P0005). Host Gottlieb Bar is a chief engineer; his later state-organizing ability is retrospective (P0017). John Woodworth is a Cortezian migrant and Maximalist; Marutzian leads his party (P0014, P0025). Arkady Gonzalez teaches art history; later cruelty is retrospective (P0015). Nikolai Pustovoyt is an accountant, later Minister of Mercy (P0016). Kazimir Shtupa is a military meteorologist; General Leonid Prishchepa is Pavel's father (P0035). Arthur Marutzian chairs Latania's government; Amin Amentola is Cortezia's president (P0134–P0135).
  • Events and relationships: Gamov predicts war the next day after Marutzian's pledge to Patina in its three-village dispute with Lamaria (P0011–P0013). He also predicts Patina's betrayal after defeat (P0059). Five gang members attack two women; Semipalov fights two at a time, Gamov bites an armed man's chin and twists his arm, and Woodworth intervenes (P0069–P0088). Semipalov cannot foresee Gamov's later punishments (P0092). Elena returns after a month apart; twelve years measures their first meeting, not a stated marriage duration (P0099–P0100, P0127).
  • World and places: Latania and Cortezia have a treaty for planned cyclone use (P0056). Shtupa predicts two days of good weather while noting hourly changes remain possible (P0038). A hurricane and broadcast follow the gathering: Patina attacks Lamaria, which asks Cortezia and Roder for aid; Cortezian overseas stations intensify operation; Latania calls men aged 18–55 (P0133–P0139).
  • Continuity: C001 records the accuracy of Gamov's predictions, not proven hidden information. C002 records the apparent forecast/attack gap pending later explanation. No confirmed contradiction. The source's title-page author spelling is an editorial issue outside plot continuity.

P1-C02 — War reversal and mobilization

  • Coverage: P0001–P0036, independently read in full. Consulted and reconciled the existing character, place, world, timeline, and continuity records.
  • Places and war: Patina captures Lama and enters Roder (the Russian nominative/accusative; Rodera is a case form). Roder has undergone thirty years of disarmament after defeat. Cortezia supplies regular troops; Latania supplies named volunteer formations. Allied leaders from Patina, Nordag, Great Lepin, Sobrana, and Torbash meet in Adan; King Knurka IX attends himself. Enemy counterattacks reverse the initial victories (P0003–P0005, P0018).
  • People and chronology: In the first months of war, institute closures give Semipalov more evenings with Elena (P0012). He is ordered to volunteer, made captain because of training three years before, and assigned by Pavel to radio sabotage in Leonid's Steel Ram division. Gamov is already major and technical deputy (P0019–P0030). Elena is mobilized for pharmaceutical synthesis in Adan; Semipalov will go west to Patina's wooded mountains. Their last evening is in Zabon (P0031–P0036).
  • World: Cyclones have unknown critical mass and volume limits; control relies on intuition (P0016). The first weather attack is repelled after one night, detected by excessive cyclone speed (P0017). Official claims of advance conflict with retreats; Semipalov openly calls broadcasts lies (P0006–P0009). Compulsory volunteering and removal of workplace buses as “voluntary donations” expose coercive political language (P0019, P0022, P0033).
  • Continuity: C002 partly resolved by P0017; no need to preserve the storm's military cause as wholly unknown. C003 is propaganda, not conflicting authorial history. Retrospective P0011 says both spouses later enter politics despite Elena's present denial; this is foreshadowing, not a contradiction.

P1-C03 — Barta and the threatened encirclement

  • Coverage: P0001–P0084, independently read in full. Consulted existing entries for the four commanders, Gonzalez, weather warfare, the two divisions, and C001–C003. Added Peano and C004; corrected inherited chronology and prediction labels.
  • Chronology and military facts: It is Semipalov's third month in Steel Ram after two months of retreat. Gamov became colonel and deputy commander three days before; Semipalov is now major and Pavel captain of reconnaissance. One month before, Golden Wings' two condensed-water cylinders exploded thirty kilometres ahead of Steel Ram, forcing it into the front line. Steel Ram now holds two hills at the Barta bend (P0002–P0010, P0024). Leonid suffers resonance injury and chills but refuses hospital evacuation (P0032–P0035).
  • People and politics: Pavel admits embellishing reports on Komlin's request. Leonid deliberately promotes Gamov's reputation as a potential future army commander (P0016–P0024). President Vilkomir Torba seeks separate peace; Gamov predicts subsequent armed defection (P0042–P0045). Major Albert Peano, 22, is Marutzian's nephew and a capable planner. Warm calls do not settle rumors of family hostility; Gamov suspects a lethal assignment (P0051–P0059). Gonzalez is a skilled but unwilling operator who hates war; he says he would hang all warmongers, including Marutzian (P0053, P0079–P0084).
  • Geography and action: Patina's third corps is left of Steel Ram; fourth and fifth are behind/right. Golden Wings is beyond the fifth; professional Latanian troops behind the third protect the Adan route. Leonid orders circular defense and reconnaissance. Semipalov requests a mobile sabotage detachment under his command (P0060–P0069).
  • Continuity: C001's Patina warning is already documented in P1-C01 P0059, not an unknown prior event. C003 is now directly confirmed propaganda. C004 preserves distinct rumor, witnessed warmth, and Gamov's suspicion about Peano. No confirmed contradiction.

P1-C04 — Shtok and the convoy

  • Coverage/consultation: P0001–P0084 complete. Consulted command, front, world, and continuity entries; searched new names and money terms before adding.
  • People: Captain Birker Shtok is Lieutenant Colonel Peter Parp's deputy. His name is real (P0013–P0045). He breaks under threatened filmed humiliation, supplies coherent information before any injection, and asks to be executed; Gamov refuses (P0026–P0071). Gonzalez eagerly assists; deliberate dirtying of the pig is narrator inference (P0027, P0040).
  • Events/world: Leonid's worsening injury leaves Gamov in practical command (P0003). Roder replaces Patina on the flanks. Shtok reports that Patina's armed fourth corps will attack Steel Ram and Golden Wings once terms are agreed (P0047). Two professional Latanian armies hold the Central Front, in Shtok's account (P0049). Parp carries 300 million kalons captured from a two-vehicle payment to Patina; his road is at least 30 km west, beyond electro-gun range (P0055–P0059).
  • Outcome/continuity: Semipalov, Pavel, and Gamov will raid that night (P0074–P0078). Gamov requests a false heroic broadcast of battles not yet fought (P0079–P0084): C003 propaganda. C001 gains supporting intelligence, not proof of armed defection. Shtok's identity dispute resolves inside the chapter. No confirmed contradiction.

P1-C05 — Raid and rewards

  • Coverage/consultation: P0001–P0123 complete. Consulted all affected commander, front, weapons, money, and continuity records before adding Korkin, Komlin, reward rules, and C005.
  • Action/technology: Inflatable suits and rafts enable the crossing; Pavel's secret Small Field Videoscope tracks the convoy (P0009–P0017). A three-group dawn ambush captures the money and 350 soldiers; enemy wounded also receive aid (P0024–P0032, P0073).
  • Numbers tied to events: Gamov's tariff: 65 enemy dead × 200 kalons = 13,000; 350 captives × 1,000 = 350,000; 200 hand vibrators plus 30 pulsators × 5,000 = 1,150,000; two electro-guns × 100,000 = 200,000. Each wounded soldier gets 2,000 and each dead soldier's family 10,000. These last groups lack counts here, so do not infer a complete payout (P0042–P0044).
  • Relationships/motives: Pavel endorses all Gamov's acts; Semipalov objects but declines open opposition. Gamov seeks motivation and political freedom through money and publicity; soldiers plan family support and threaten resistance to confiscation (P0021–P0023, P0050–P0093). Leonid neither approves nor revokes the payout (P0097).
  • Front and outcome: General Filipp Korkin's Golden Wings is attacked by Roder's second army from south/east and pressed against the sea. Marshal Anton Komlin's dispatch, signed Western Front commander, admits its collapse but orders Steel Ram to stay. Leonid requests rescue permission; Semipalov prepares dismantling (P0100–P0123).
  • Continuity: C003 publicity is purposeful. C005 explicitly records profitable combat versus the aim of making war repulsive; it is a debated doctrine, not authorial inconsistency. No confirmed contradiction.

P1-C06 — Mutiny and formal doctrine

  • Coverage/consultation: P0001–P0105 complete. Consulted reward, humiliation, front, commander, and C005 entries; searched Serbin/Varella before adding.
  • Events/people: Golden Wings surrenders by morning (P0002–P0003). Private Semyon Serbin of the second battalion leads a cash demand; raider Varella challenges and subdues him. Semipalov prevents guards from firing. Gamov substitutes latrine humiliation for execution and orders washing afterward (P0012–P0054). His dark mood is concern about encirclement, not established shame (P0052–P0056).
  • Plan: Semipalov proposes freeing captive columns about 50 km away by crossing west, then returning east as a two-division corps. Leonid accepts despite Komlin's hold order (P0058–P0068). Semipalov drops his reward objection to preserve morale (P0073–P0074).
  • Exact tariff: Capture/destruction in kalons: waterplane 2m/1m; electro-gun 200k/100k; hand vibrator 10k/5k; motor vehicle 20k/10k; weather generator 4m/2m. Capture/kill: general 500k/100k; other officer 50k/10k; soldier 1k/200. Wound 2k; dead soldier's heirs 10k (P0084–P0102). This deliberately replaces the improvised tariff (P0075).
  • Continuity/outcome: Gonzalez favors dead enemies; Gamov defends capture as preserving coerced human beings, discouraging revenge, and encouraging surrender. Peano and Semipalov agree (P0079–P0082). All four followers now support new methods, not yet their full ultimate scope (P0105). C005 remains ideological tension; no confirmed contradiction.

P1-C07 — Liberation and corps appointments

  • Coverage/consultation: P0001–P0101 complete. Consulted affected command, rewards, front, technology, and C003–C005 records before additions.
  • Politics/technology: Leonid refuses a permanent tariff; Gamov signs (P0003–P0005). Leonid plants a false relief rumor, later replaced by Gamov's true mission briefing (P0012, P0026). Pavel lends secret paired transmitter 77, claims interception immunity, then reclaims it (P0023–P0025, P0069–P0072). Three waterplanes are seen; Cortezia has begun mass production (P0040). A resonance-struck tree's survival remains unknown (P0038).
  • Action/chronology: Prisoners travel in three columns, reportedly 30 leagues/day for three days (P0014–P0018; do not convert). Barrage starts 20:15; Semipalov crosses 21:00–22:30 (P0037–P0038), reaches the road next night, meets the escort the following evening, and holds through the next day until Gamov's heavy guns induce surrender (P0050–P0065). Pavel's rear attack raises morale but does not divert the main enemy assault (P0063–P0064).
  • Command/outcome: Freed Golden Wings retains divisional manpower. Leonid appoints Gamov corps commander, himself retains Steel Ram, Korkin becomes his deputy, Semipalov takes Golden Wings; higher approval is pending (P0075–P0095). One organization day precedes the planned return offensive (P0085–P0086). Gamov's proposed public report names the appointments/rewards and blames headquarters (P0096–P0099).
  • Continuity: C003's invented defense is used to deny rescue, explicitly condemned by Leonid; actual breakout battles are later. C004 leaves Peano's present thoughts unknown despite later loyalty. No confirmed contradiction.

P1-C08 — Serbin restored; Mordasov killed

  • Coverage/consultation: P0001–P0103 complete. Consulted Serbin, command, Peano, weapons, money, and continuity records; searched Mordasov before addition.
  • Serbin: Shows rewards for two resonators, an officer, and a wound; Gamov embraces him before an ecstatic crowd (P0008–P0011). The narrator speculates about collective absolution and foreshadows Serbin's later sinister influence without specifying its form (P0012–P0013): C006.
  • Envoy: Danilo Mordasov represents Marutzian/Komlin. Called general initially, he corrects this to state councillor (P0017, P0038). Reports 200,000 Western Front prisoners, supply/crop failure, desertion and banditry (P0034–P0046). Demands all remaining and distributed cash; offers possible command confirmation if Gamov complies (P0053–P0056).
  • Clash: Peano alleges intended embezzlement. Mordasov quotes Marutzian's conditional arrest order against him (P0072–P0077): C004 partly clarified, prior suspected murder plot unproven. Gamov sentences Mordasov; Gonzalez's pulsator kills him as he draws a resonator (P0083–P0085).
  • Outcome: Gamov's proposed report casts the real envoy as a saboteur and repeats leadership criticism as slander; Pavel has recorded the meeting. Leonid pledges total support (P0093–P0103). C003 propaganda, C006 interpretation; no confirmed contradiction.

P1-C09 — Return and offer of supreme power

  • Coverage/consultation: P0001–P0115 complete. Consulted existing people, corps, money, technology, and continuity entries; searched Serov/Parku before additions.
  • Time/action: One week of battle ends in breakout; P0003 also says several hundred hours, creating C007 numerical tension (P0002–P0004). Rewards exhaust both cash vehicles. Ten-day celebrated return follows (P0005, P0034–P0036).
  • People: Sergeant Serov joins Serbin and Grigory Varella in a protective delegation. Varella's first name and role as Pavel's man are now explicit. Pavel, Peano, and Gonzalez organized soldiers without informing Gamov/Semipalov (P0006–P0032). Elena reunites at Parku through Woodworth's pass; he is now Marutzian's foreign-affairs adviser (P0036–P0047).
  • Disclosures: Woodworth preserved unit structure, weapons, and loyal guards against a plan to disperse the commanders and dissolve the corps (P0062–P0079). Pavel confirms the three staff officers isolated Mordasov's guards before the interview without senior commanders' knowledge (P0072–P0074). Woodworth says the cash was allocated to secret government bonuses, including his, and Peano was to be removed; prison is his inference (P0075).
  • Outcome/world: Woodworth asks Gamov to take supreme power (P0082); Pavel has already replaced Woodworth's guards (P0089–P0091). Semipalov/staff begin planning with Gamov's tacit allowance (P0100–P0108). Transport shortage forces locomotives back to coal/oil (P0056). Gamov is unmarried; his later jealousy question foreshadows an unspecified future plan (P0054, P0109–P0115): C008.
  • Continuity: C004 strengthened, but prior murder plot remains unproved. C007 is numerical ambiguity, not confirmed plot error. C008 stays open; no affair established.

P1-C10 — Coup and transfer of power

  • Coverage/consultation: P0001–P0125 complete. Consulted all affected prior government, command, place, technology, and continuity entries; searched Morokhov before adding. Part 1 total: 1,015 source paragraphs across all ten chapters.
  • Coup: The officers travel Zabon–Adan under a reporting summons; Semipalov authored the operational plan. Soldiers are positioned in civilian dress. Pavel reports seizure of railway/broadcast stations and heavy vibrators at security barracks; telephone and palace communications then fail. Colonel Morokhov, government security chief, cannot restore control (P0006–P0016, P0039–P0044, P0053–P0084).
  • World: Shtupa reports Cortezian weather stations in Roder/Lamaria and seasonal denial/flooding strategy. Latania obtained spring rain but expects a wet, reduced harvest; civilians face further ration cuts (P0028–P0034). Pavel's larger secret device has about a hundred sevens and written messages (P0039).
  • Evidence and people: Gamov displays a secret allocation document: two million kalons for Komlin (P0065–P0067). Marutzian says Korkin was demoted (P0051). Varella's armed party, including Serov and Serbin, occupies the chamber and strips Komlin's insignia (P0084–P0088). Peano threatens his uncle; Marutzian regrets sending him to the front rather than prison (P0097–P0101): hostile relationship confirmed, attempted killing still unproved.
  • Outcome: Woodworth provides a prepared resignation and camera crew. Marutzian's coerced broadcast transfers power to nonparty Colonel Gamov. Gamov accepts, postpones cabinet/program, and thanks Semipalov. Semipalov orders former ministers removed (P0103–P0125). His impression of Komlin's permanent mental collapse is explicitly corrected by hindsight (P0122).
  • Continuity/handoff: C001 unexplained precision; C002 attack partly explained; C003 deliberate propaganda; C004 hostility confirmed but lethal-intent suspicion unproved; C005 ideological tension; C006 Serbin future influence; C007 one-week/hundreds-hours ambiguity; C008 unspecified jealousy plan. No confirmed plot contradiction in Part 1. Central facts stop at this chapter; no Part 2 read.

Final cross-part dispositions

The central register is reconciled through P6-C15. C002's storm is explained as an attack; C004 confirms hostility without proving the murder-intent suspicion. C006's Serbin foreshadowing is fulfilled by his guard/broadcast/intervention roles, while crowd psychology remains uncertain. C008 resolves through the staged triangle and later marital reunion. C001's precise information source and C007's rhetorical battle-hour tension remain unresolved. Later Gamov rank and Parp/Porp wording are recorded in C011. The early anti-war aim at P1-C05 P0059 remains explicit. The independent review and final timeline subject correction preserve Gonzalez as the actor giving Peano the ominous response.
