# Places and political geography

[Catalog index](index.md). Keep location identity separate from changing political control. Record uncertain distances, directions, and routes as uncertain.

<a id="latania"></a>
## Latania

Semipalov's country and the setting of Bar's Thursday gathering. Bar lives in a three-room apartment on the outskirts. Police maintain tolerable order on central avenues, while gangs control neighborhood territory at night. Gamov describes Latania as unprepared and politically foolish in the face of Cortezian power. Source: [P1-C01](source-map.md#p1-c01).

<a id="cortezia"></a>
## Cortezia

A major foreign power and Woodworth's former homeland. Gamov calls the Cortezians predators and predicts that their meteorological stations are being forced into action. The news later reports that Cortezia's overseas stations enter enhanced operation and that its fleet leaves port. Source: [P1-C01](source-map.md#p1-c01).

<a id="patina"></a>
## Patina

State in a long dispute with Lamaria over three border villages. Marutzian promises support for Patina; Patina then attacks Lamaria. Gamov calls Patina an ally whose leaders may use Latania's support for their own interests. Source: [P1-C01](source-map.md#p1-c01).

<a id="lamaria"></a>
## Lamaria

State opposing Patina in the border dispute. Its capital is Lama. The news reports that Lamaria requests help from Cortezia and Roder after Patina's attack. Source: [P1-C01](source-map.md#p1-c01).

<a id="lama"></a>
## Lama

Capital of Lamaria. The news says Patina's army is advancing toward Lama after breaking Lamaria's defenses. Source: [P1-C01](source-map.md#p1-c01).

<a id="rodera"></a>
<a id="roder"></a>
## Roder

State named in the broadcast as another power from which Lamaria requests help. The Russian nominative is Roder; Rodera is its genitive case, not another state. Roder is Cortezia's principal ally and an old military nation that disarmed for three decades after losing its last major war. Patina crosses its border early in the war; Cortezian troops and weapons arrive through Roder's ports, and the combined force later drives Patina and Latanian volunteers back. Source: [P1-C01](source-map.md#p1-c01), [P1-C02](source-map.md#p1-c02), P0003.

<a id="bar-apartment"></a>
## Gottlieb Bar's apartment

Three-room apartment in an unspecified Latanian district, large enough for three armchairs and six chairs. It is the site of the first political debate and Semipalov's first sustained exposure to Gamov's ideas. Source: [P1-C01](source-map.md#p1-c01).

<a id="latanian-outskirts"></a>
## Latanian outskirts at night

Poorly policed neighborhood where five gang members attack two women. Semipalov and Gamov leave the scene after a violent fight; Woodworth arrives from a nearby street. Source: [P1-C01](source-map.md#p1-c01).

<a id="adan"></a>
## Adan

Capital of Latania and site of the allied conference, later the destination of Elena's pharmaceutical mobilization. Source: [P1-C02](source-map.md#p1-c02).

<a id="nordag"></a>
## Nordag

Friendly state represented at the allied conference in Adan. No further geography is supplied. Source: [P1-C02](source-map.md#p1-c02).

<a id="great-lepin"></a>
## Great Lepin

Friendly state represented at the allied conference in Adan. No further geography is supplied. Source: [P1-C02](source-map.md#p1-c02).

<a id="sobrana"></a>
## Sobrana

Friendly state represented at the allied conference in Adan. No further geography is supplied. Source: [P1-C02](source-map.md#p1-c02).

<a id="torbash"></a>
## Torbash

Small state described as lacking an army and industry. Its hereditary king, Knurka IX, attends the Adan conference in person. Source: [P1-C02](source-map.md#p1-c02).

<a id="zabon"></a>
## Zabon

City where Semipalov and Elena spend their final evening before wartime assignments. The city is blacked out; street lamps have been removed and only four interior lights remain in their apartment. Source: [P1-C02](source-map.md#p1-c02).

<a id="patina-wooded-mountains"></a>
## Patina's wooded mountains

Western, forested region of Patina where Semipalov is ordered to serve after mobilization. Source: [P1-C02](source-map.md#p1-c02).

<a id="steel-ram-division"></a>
## Steel Ram division

Latanian volunteer division commanded by General Leonid Prishchepa. It uses radio-pulse devices, resonance guns, and electro-artillery; Semipalov joins as a radio saboteur. Source: [P1-C02](source-map.md#p1-c02).

<a id="barta-river"></a>
## Barta River and wooded hills

A winding river dividing Latanian forces from Roder. Steel Ram holds two wooded hills above a bend in the river and builds its main electro-barrier there after two months of retreat. Source: [P1-C03](source-map.md#p1-c03).

<a id="golden-wings-division"></a>
## Golden Wings division

Volunteer division thirty kilometres ahead of Steel Ram on the main defense line. Its condensed-water energy depot explodes, causing panic and exposing the front to Roder's guards. Source: [P1-C03](source-map.md#p1-c03).

<a id="steel-ram-front"></a>
## Steel Ram forward position

A nearly encircled position after Patina requests separate peace. Patina corps stand behind and on both flanks, Golden Wings lies behind them, and professional Latanian units rebuild a line protecting the road to Adan. Source: [P1-C03](source-map.md#p1-c03).

### P1-C04 front updates

Roder replaces Patina's third and fifth corps on Steel Ram's flanks; the fourth remains in the rear. Shtok reports a plan for the fourth to attack the two volunteer divisions after a new agreement. A road at least thirty kilometres west of Steel Ram carries Parp's money convoy. The Central Front is held by two professional Latanian armies against Cortezia, according to Shtok. Source: [P1-C04](source-map.md#p1-c04), P0002–P0004, P0047–P0059.

### P1-C05 front updates

Roder's second army attacks Golden Wings from south and east after replacing Patina's fifth corps. Golden Wings' rear is pressed against the sea, where no enemy ships are yet visible. Its last line is threatened and unified command collapses, according to the respective Korkin and Komlin reports. Source: [P1-C05](source-map.md#p1-c05), P0100–P0106.

### P1-C06 rescue route

Golden Wings surrenders. Its captive columns are expected to pass roughly fifty kilometres from Steel Ram. Semipalov proposes crossing west over the Barta to free them, then returning east with the combined force. Northern/southern enemy forces and Patina's fourth corps in the east make the westward route the best available exit; this is an approved plan, not yet completed movement. Source: [P1-C06](source-map.md#p1-c06), P0003–P0008, P0058–P0068.

<a id="volunteer-corps"></a>
## Combined volunteer corps

Steel Ram frees Golden Wings beside an unnamed evacuated village and surrounding hills on the captive columns' road. The two divisions form a corps under Gamov, with appointments initially made in the field. Two enemy corps converge from the east and from across the Barta; Gamov plans to strike before they combine. Source: [P1-C07](source-map.md#p1-c07), P0050–P0054, P0065–P0096.

### P1-C08 reported conditions in Latania

Mordasov reports 200,000 prisoners lost on the Western Front, drought/crop damage from Cortezian interception of cyclones, diverted food reserves, desertion, and violent banditry in Adan. These are his nationwide claims; the narrator is still in the corps. Source: [P1-C08](source-map.md#p1-c08), P0034–P0046.

<a id="parku"></a>
## Parku

Rail junction reached by road during the corps' return. Lines lead to Zabon and Adan. Elena meets Semipalov here using Woodworth's pass; civilians are otherwise barred. The officers board Woodworth's train with the loyal raiding battalion. Source: [P1-C09](source-map.md#p1-c09), P0036–P0042, P0062.

### P1-C10 Adan and strategic geography

The party travels from Zabon to Adan. The narrator observes closed shops, queues, and the absence of evacuated children. Pavel's men seize the railway and broadcast stations, surround security barracks with heavy vibrators, and cut city/palace communication as soldiers occupy the government chamber. Shtupa reports new Cortezian weather stations under construction in Roder and Lamaria. Source: [P1-C10](source-map.md#p1-c10), P0006–P0012, P0028, P0039–P0044, P0056–P0084.

## Part 2 — Adan, Zabon, and the northern war

Semipalov and Elena now stay in an Adan hotel while their Zabon home is locked. This is a wartime residence change, not a change in the identity of either city. [P2-C02](source-map.md#p2-c02), P0002.


### P2-C03 — Palace and intended front

Gamov's smaller semicircular council room adjoins his living rooms and private “confessional.” Peano describes the intended real offensive on the southern sector of the Western Front, toward lost areas of Lamaria and Patina; the false leak instead names the north near Zabon. These are plans at this point. [P2-C03](source-map.md#p2-c03), P0033–P0037, P0094–P0099.

### P2-C05 — Conference geography

The visitors stay at Adan's Podnebesnaya hotel. Korina is then neutral, across a narrow strait from northern Roder. Zabon is Latania's second center; its defenses can resist several divisions, but Semipalov doubts they can stop several corps. The earlier southern offensive is now described as a central attack toward Lamaria and Patina; see [C010](continuity.md#c010). [P2-C05](source-map.md#p2-c05), P0005–P0011, P0077–P0091.


### P2-C06 — Zabon and the rejected strategic sacrifice

The enemy attacks Zabon after finding no northern Latanian concentration. Semipalov proposes abandoning the city, breaking the weakened center, and encircling its captors from the west. Gamov instead orders its defense and an immediate central offensive. Woodworth warns that enemy corps near Nordag could provoke its defection. These proposed alternatives must remain separate from the eventual campaign. [P2-C06](source-map.md#p2-c06), P0017–P0035.


### P2-C07 — Zabon encircled

Hills arc from the east around Zabon toward the northern sea; the southwest approach is low and marshy. Nordag lies northeast. A failed flood attack delays Vaksel but also inundates Zabon and Nordag's border districts. Nordag then overwhelms the border defenses and reaches Zabon's northeast outskirts. [P2-C07](source-map.md#p2-c07), P0021–P0026, P0043, P0089–P0103.


### P2-C09 — The blockade ends

Nordag holds Zabon's outer airfield and food depots beyond narrow gorges. Its withdrawal reopens the gorge; restored road contact with Adan is expected by evening. This observed relief precedes any completed peace treaty. The captives are displayed in a glass-walled enclosure with an opaque roof, implementing the prior cage order with different materials. [P2-C09](source-map.md#p2-c09), P0002–P0005, P0052–P0061, P0077–P0085.


## Part 3 — Autumn to the new offensive

- **P3-C01:** Peano reports Zabon relieved, but two-thirds of Patina abandoned and Lamaria still unrecovered. Nordag's reported withdrawal line is thirty leagues inland; no modern conversion is supplied. [Evidence](parts/part-3-ledger.md#p3-c01), P0018, P0031–P0035.
- **P3-C02:** Operation Cleansing covers Latania's cities, forests, and mountain refuges. Its end of organized banditry is Gamov's announcement; isolated crime is explicitly allowed to persist. [Evidence](parts/part-3-ledger.md#p3-c02), P0003–P0015.
- **P3-C03:** Semipalov requests a bank channel in Klur; the country is explicitly neutral in **P3-C04**, when Biber and Falk visit Adan. Their visit does not make Klur a Latanian ally. [P3-C03](parts/part-3-ledger.md#p3-c03), P0089–P0105; [P3-C04](parts/part-3-ledger.md#p3-c04), P0025–P0026.

### P3-C05–P3-C08 — Neutrality ends and Konduk falls

- **P3-C05:** Orion bank in neutral Klur holds the hundred offered accounts. Konda is a place visited by Kirun; its wider status is unstated. The secret aircraft plant occupies an unnamed mountain reached from Adan through forest and a gorge. [Evidence](parts/part-3-ledger.md#p3-c05), P0014, P0022, P0043–P0048.
- **P3-C06:** The first natural spring thunderstorm over Adan accompanies the enemy peace terms. Their territorial demands are demands, not completed border changes. [Evidence](parts/part-3-ledger.md#p3-c06), P0002–P0005.
- **P3-C07:** Floria is Latania's western autonomous region beside Patina. One week into Vaksel's offensive, Great Lepin, Sobrana, Konduk, Klur, and Korina declare war. Klur and Korina therefore cease to be neutral. Torbash remains formally out. Konduk bombs peaceful desert Sorbas; the early half-dead/half-escaped estimate is preliminary reporting. [Evidence](parts/part-3-ledger.md#p3-c07), P0038–P0041, P0052–P0053, P0077–P0088.
- **P3-C08:** Kondina is Konduk's capital, seized by airborne troops while its army still fights at the border. Ground forces follow and an occupation government forms. Do not merge Kondina with Konda. Klur sends two divisions to Vaksel; Korina sends one to Nordag. Woodworth counters Torbash's demands with requests for roads and paid food supplies. [Evidence](parts/part-3-ledger.md#p3-c08), P0010–P0035, P0046–P0077.


### P3-C09–P3-C13 — Renewed pressure and hidden bases

Vaksel advances from Patina into Floria; Nordag again blocks Zabon from north and east. Artificial storms damage the approaches to Adan. [P3-C09](source-map.md#p3-c09), P0002–P0005. The unnamed mutinous airbase has six barracks and underground hangars for 120 operational aircraft; it is one of many divisions. [P3-C10](source-map.md#p3-c10), P0002–P0009, P0047–P0059. Bernoulli's neutral transit country is not identified as now-belligerent Klur. [P3-C11](source-map.md#p3-c11), P0009. Semipalov's public hanging is in Adan at noon. [P3-C13](source-map.md#p3-c13), P0002.


## Part 4 — Recovery, conquest, and coercive occupation

- **P4-C01–P4-C03:** Semipalov's guarded park and nearby laboratory have no stated locality. Floria's reported loss advances from half to all; the enemy then enters ethnic Latanian territory. Sorbas is also where Kozyura says he was found. [P4-C01](parts/part-4-ledger.md#p4-c01), P0011, P0021; [P4-C03](parts/part-4-ledger.md#p4-c03), P0024, P0028–P0035.
- **P4-C02:** The screen shows an unnamed other-world city, then an unnamed nuclear attack elsewhere or elsewhen. No city, country, Earth identity, or historical bombing name is given. Semipalov compares the very tall buildings with his own cities' houses. [Evidence](parts/part-4-ledger.md#p4-c02), P0066–P0101.
- **P4-C04–P4-C05:** Fermor is Klur's capital and conference site; delegates lodge in the five-story Svetoch hotel. The principal offensive targets prisoner camps in Patina, Lamaria, and Roder, while the conference raid is secondary. [P4-C04](parts/part-4-ledger.md#p4-c04), P0002, P0031–P0035; [P4-C05](parts/part-4-ledger.md#p4-c05), P0025–P0047.


### P4-C06–P4-C10 — Occupation limits

The Fermor force evacuates its captives and leaves Klur within a week, without destroying its fortifications. Freed prisoners are too weak to fight at once, so supplies and six unnamed defensive towns become essential. Less than a month after the raid, Patina, Lamaria, and Roder are described as conquered; Latanian troops stop at Roder's Klur border. [P4-C06](source-map.md#p4-c06), P0002–P0012; [P4-C07](source-map.md#p4-c07), P0004–P0017, P0066–P0068.

In autumn, returning Latanians receive thirty days' leave; captured enemy soldiers go to rear camps. [P4-C08](source-map.md#p4-c08), P0002–P0004. Floria is made formally independent and neutral, with population transfers, compensation, withdrawn subsidies, and bypass roads. Its expected economic collapse and return are forecasts. Laina is Patina's capital. [P4-C09](source-map.md#p4-c09), P0011–P0027, P0053–P0057. Cortezia's capital is named Kordaza; later source spelling Kordoza denotes the same city. [P4-C10](source-map.md#p4-c10), P0007; [P4-C11](source-map.md#p4-c11), P0034.


### P4-C11–P4-C15 — The execution flight and Nordag

The reprisal crosses Patina, Lamaria, Roder, Klur, and the ocean to Cortezia; hostages are released in Klur on return. Kordoza and earlier Kordaza are source spellings of Cortezia's capital. [P4-C11](source-map.md#p4-c11), P0024, P0034, P0043–P0049. After the public welcome, Klur resumes military patrols: a crowd's response did not establish peace. [P4-C12](source-map.md#p4-c12), P0002–P0008.

Nordag's city and army supply systems are isolated; Latanian forces initially stay outside the defended settlements. Parko is its capital, distinct from Latania's Parku rail junction. Diverted rain inundates Korina and Klur. Semipalov arrives after Parko capitulates, on the day before Luiza's threatened execution at noon on the first day of leaf-fall. The fictional month is not converted to a Gregorian date. [P4-C15](source-map.md#p4-c15), P0002–P0011, P0033–P0058.


## Part 5 — Epidemic, famine, and changing alliances

- **P5-C01:** Floria's lost supplies and closed borders are effects of the earlier separation policy; Semipalov confirms that they were intended. The attacker's explanation is political grievance. Korina's epidemic threatens movement through Nordag toward Latania, but that spread is not yet observed. [Evidence](parts/part-5-ledger.md#p5-c01), P0011–P0014, P0048–P0065.
- **P5-C02–P5-C04:** Sporadic water allergy becomes epidemic in Korina. Suspected then detected Klur cases precede the text's statement that no epidemic has entered it. The rescue plan reaches the coast through Patina, Lamaria, and Nordag. Korina accepts enemy help; Klur eventually permits the weather program. [P5-C02](parts/part-5-ledger.md#p5-c02), P0054–P0066; [P5-C03](parts/part-5-ledger.md#p5-c03), P0004–P0017; [P5-C04](parts/part-5-ledger.md#p5-c04), P0010–P0018.
- **P5-C05:** After months, Latania avoids epidemic, but Nordag, Patina, and Klur suffer deaths; Klur's coast is devastated. No rain falls from Adan to the ocean through summer. This corrects earlier optimistic hopes, not an earlier established universal rescue. [Evidence](parts/part-5-ledger.md#p5-c05), P0002–P0003.
- **P5-C06:** Cortezia stops exports. Klur and Korina ration food; Amentola demands concessions. In Fermor, women seize palace and broadcast sites; gendarmes crush the revolt, then Pliss restores authoritarian control. Loaded Cortezian food remains blocked by ocean storms. [Evidence](parts/part-5-ledger.md#p5-c06), P0005–P0071.


### P5-C07–P5-C11 — Aid routes and the end of this war

Anna's campaign produces high milk yields in Floria without ending its hostility to Semipalov. [P5-C07](source-map.md#p5-c07), P0003–P0004. Food travels through Patina, Lamaria, and Roder to Klur's frontier, then enters under armed salutes and civilian welcome; soldiers initially retain their positions. The narrative gives a day's travel and a halt over half a day, but retrospectively returns to the voting day's noon. [P5-C09](source-map.md#p5-c09), P0027–P0047; [P5-C10](source-map.md#p5-c10), P0018–P0047.

A limited weather corridor lets small boats carry Nordag's food to Korina. It does not make the entire ocean navigable for Cortezia's distant ships. Klur's parade crosses the Alley of Victories, also called Alley of Feats, to the Square of Greatness and Triumphal Arch. [P5-C10](source-map.md#p5-c10), P0030–P0038, P0054, P0069–P0092. Cortezia's final mass strike and palace marches bring a peace announcement; no Latanian transoceanic invasion occurs. [P5-C11](source-map.md#p5-c11), P0022, P0087–P0133.


## Part 6 — World settlement and the narrator's final boundary

The survivor conference meets in Adan's hall where Gamov suffered the heart attack. Natural weather is deliberate: he orders no generator use. [P6-C01](source-map.md#p6-c01), P0010–P0015. Adan becomes the temporary world capital after the rulers approve union; they return home to reorganize their states. The detailed national changes are not all narrated. [P6-C03](source-map.md#p6-c03), P0004–P0006. Biber/Falk's earlier Fermor capture is later described more broadly as during Tronshke's army's collapse; see C029.


### P6-C11–P6-C15 — Two worlds and the knowledge boundary

Sorbas remains the place near which Gamov recalls rescue; Tarkhun's negative caravan search is reported evidence, not a named birthplace in another world. [P6-C11](source-map.md#p6-c11), P0045–P0108. The departure occurs at the same laboratory, but its later homeland history becomes inaccessible to the narrator. [P6-C14](source-map.md#p6-c14), P0047–P0049.

Semipalov wakes in an unnamed foreign state's military center, later learning he is on floor 128. Nearby structures are roughly 180–200 floors. Neither city nor state nor historical date is named. The cabin reportedly appeared at a football goal. The final guarded government meeting begins one floor above his confinement. The authorial 1986–1987, Kaliningrad–Komarovo colophon is outside story chronology. [P6-C15](source-map.md#p6-c15), P0002–P0025, P0038, P0053–P0064.
