# Timeline and events

[Catalog index](index.md). Use explicit dates only when the text supplies them. Keep narrative order, chronological order, recollection, prediction, and stated duration distinct. Use one stable chapter anchor per entry.

<a id="p1-c01"></a>
## P1-C01 — Thursday evening to the next morning

- **Thursday evening, at Bar's gathering:** Gamov predicts that Marutzian's promise to support Patina will start a world war the next day. Bar and Shtupa see no material or meteorological preparation. Semipalov hears Gamov's anti-war speech and becomes psychologically ready to follow him. Source: [P1-C01](source-map.md#p1-c01).
- **Later that night:** Semipalov and Gamov encounter five gang members attacking two women on the Latanian outskirts. Woodworth joins the fight. Gamov uses extreme violence, then releases the injured men after threatening them with his name. Source: [P1-C01](source-map.md#p1-c01).
- **After midnight:** Semipalov reaches home and finds Elena asleep on the sofa. She has been away for one month and says she cannot live without him, although living with him is difficult. They first met twelve years earlier; the marriage duration is unstated. Source: [P1-C01](source-map.md#p1-c01).
- **Before dawn, after Semipalov falls asleep:** An unforecast hurricane strikes Latania. News reports Patina's attack on Lamaria, Lamaria's requests to Cortezia and Roder, Cortezia's enhanced weather operations, and Marutzian's volunteer mobilization for ages eighteen to fifty-five. The power fails during the broadcast. Source: [P1-C01](source-map.md#p1-c01).

**Current state after P1-C01:** War has begun between Patina and Lamaria. Cortezia has moved toward intervention. Latania's weather and political systems have entered an emergency state. Gamov's warning is confirmed in outcome, but his information source and the storm's mechanism remain unresolved; see [C001](continuity.md#c001) and [C002](continuity.md#c002).

<a id="p1-c02"></a>
## P1-C02 — Early war, reversals, and mobilization

- **Early war:** Patina defeats Lamaria, captures Lama, and crosses into Roder. Latania forms volunteer divisions but keeps its army at the border; Cortezia sends regular troops and weapons into Roder. Source: [P1-C02](source-map.md#p1-c02).
- **Allied conference:** Heads of friendly states meet in Adan. Marutzian gives three speeches each day, and the coalition celebrates a promised victory. After the conference ends, enemy forces counterattack and the front begins to reverse. Source: [P1-C02](source-map.md#p1-c02).
- **First months of war and home-front conditions:** The institute closes at night. Meat is removed from the monthly ration because authorities fear a second meteorological attack and crop failure. Elena and Semipalov spend more evenings together. Source: [P1-C02](source-map.md#p1-c02).
- **Meteorological attack:** Latania's generators repel a surprise cyclone after one night, but the combined Cortezian–Roder army retakes Lama and drives Patina's forces back. Source: [P1-C02](source-map.md#p1-c02).
- **Mobilization:** Semipalov becomes a volunteer captain and radio saboteur. Elena is sent to synthesize medicine in Adan. Pavel assigns Semipalov to the Steel Ram division under General Leonid Prishchepa; Gamov is now a major and deputy technical officer. Source: [P1-C02](source-map.md#p1-c02).
- **Final evening before departure:** Zabon is blacked out; robberies continue. Elena and Semipalov share a rare calm before she leaves for Adan and he leaves for Patina's wooded mountains. Source: [P1-C02](source-map.md#p1-c02).

**Current state after P1-C02:** The war has turned against Patina and Latania's volunteers. Weather control has shown both offensive danger and technical limits. Semipalov, Elena, Pavel, and Gamov have moved from civilian life into distinct wartime roles.

<a id="p1-c03"></a>
## P1-C03 — The Barta defense and Patina's withdrawal

- **Third month in the division:** Steel Ram has retreated for two months and now holds two wooded hills over the Barta River. Semipalov completes the main electro-barrier and secures condensed-water energy stores. Source: [P1-C03](source-map.md#p1-c03).
- **Earlier battlefield loss:** The Golden Wings division's energy depot explodes, causing a panic retreat and exposing the line to Roder's guards. Leonid Prishchepa keeps Steel Ram's front continuous and defies a withdrawal order from Commander-in-Chief Komlin. Source: [P1-C03](source-map.md#p1-c03).
- **Command changes:** Gamov becomes colonel and deputy division commander; Semipalov and Pavel become majors and captains respectively. Pavel commands reconnaissance, and Semipalov forms a mobile sabotage detachment. Source: [P1-C03](source-map.md#p1-c03).
- **Patina's separate peace:** A radio message reports Torba's order to stop fighting. The Steel Ram map shows Patina corps behind and on both flanks, Golden Wings behind them, and professional Latanian units guarding the road to Adan. Leonid orders circular defense. Source: [P1-C03](source-map.md#p1-c03).
- **Political foreshadowing:** Albert Peano's family relationship with Marutzian is disputed by rumor and observed behavior. Gonzalez answers Peano's joke with an ominous look and threat. Source: [P1-C03](source-map.md#p1-c03), P0079–P0084.

**Current state after P1-C03:** Steel Ram is nearly trapped but has a prepared barrier and a mobile sabotage plan. Patina is leaving the war. Gamov's public reputation is being built while his command power grows.

<a id="p1-c04"></a>
## P1-C04 — Interrogation and raid plan

At a spring midday, Roder takes the vacated flanks while Leonid's illness leaves Gamov in practical command. Captured Captain Shtok yields to a threat of filmed humiliation. He reports Patina's intended armed defection and Parp's convoy of three hundred million kalons. Semipalov, Pavel, and Gamov plan to raid it that night. Gamov orders advance propaganda about battles not yet fought. Source: [P1-C04](source-map.md#p1-c04), P0002–P0007, P0026–P0084.

<a id="p1-c05"></a>
## P1-C05 — Night crossing, dawn raid, evening return

The raiders cross at night, halt near Parp at about three, and attack at dawn. They take the money, 350 captives, and weapons. Gamov awards captured cash to the soldiers and explains publicity as protection from command interference. Semipalov objects to profitable war but does not openly resist. They return to the Barta late that evening. Golden Wings is collapsing; Leonid requests authority to help and allows preparations while awaiting an answer. Source: [P1-C05](source-map.md#p1-c05), P0017–P0123.

<a id="p1-c06"></a>
## P1-C06 — Surrender, mutiny, and a western breakout plan

By the next morning Golden Wings has surrendered. A crowd demands cash; Serbin's attempted rush ends in humiliating punishment without a reported shooting. Semipalov proposes freeing the captive division by a westward breakout; Leonid approves despite orders to hold. The officers formalize reward rates and debate preservation of enemy lives. Source: [P1-C06](source-map.md#p1-c06), P0002–P0105.

<a id="p1-c07"></a>
## P1-C07 — Breakout and rescue

The price list is published over Leonid's objection. Planning intelligence says captive columns have traveled thirty leagues daily for three days; no conversion to kilometres is supplied (P0018). The breakout barrage begins at 20:15; Semipalov crosses from 21:00 to 22:30. He reaches the road the next night, fights the following evening, and holds through the next day's assault until Gamov's guns arrive. The escort surrenders; Golden Wings is freed. Gamov becomes field commander of the combined corps, Semipalov its second divisional commander, pending approval. One day is assigned to organization before an eastward offensive. Source: [P1-C07](source-map.md#p1-c07), P0036–P0096.

<a id="p1-c08"></a>
## P1-C08 — Reward day and Mordasov's death

Rewards are distributed from dawn to midday. Serbin publicly reconciles with Gamov after earning a reward. That evening Mordasov meets headquarters at eight, demands the money, threatens Peano's arrest, and is killed by Gonzalez on Gamov's order. Gamov dictates a carefully framed public defense; Leonid pledges full support. Source: [P1-C08](source-map.md#p1-c08), P0002–P0103.

<a id="p1-c09"></a>
## P1-C09 — Breakout completed, homeward march, and coup proposal

The next week is described as one continuous battle ending in escape to friendly lines; the simultaneous “several hundred hours” description creates [C007](continuity.md#c007). All captured money is then distributed. A soldier delegation pledges resistance to disarmament. Ten days of homeward celebrations lead to Parku and the train, where Woodworth discloses government containment plans, admits the secret bonus allocation, and proposes Gamov's accession. Semipalov and the staff begin planning while Gamov withholds a formal reply. Source: [P1-C09](source-map.md#p1-c09), P0002–P0108.

<a id="p1-c10"></a>
## P1-C10 — Adan coup

After preparation in Zabon, the conspirators travel overnight to Adan under a reporting summons. During the morning government meeting, Pavel's forces secure key sites and cut communications while Gamov accuses the leaders. Varella's detachment occupies the chamber. Peano's threat forces Marutzian to accept a prepared broadcast transfer; Gamov becomes head of government. Former ministers are removed. No date or intervening Zabon duration is specified. Source: [P1-C10](source-map.md#p1-c10), P0006–P0125.

<a id="p2-c01"></a>
## P2-C01 — One week after the change of government

Cortezian–Roderan weather attacks flood Adan. On the meeting day, Latanian meteorologists move the rain 100 leagues away. The six rulers agree on reserves, production incentives, alliance withdrawal, public humiliation, and confession-based amnesty; Gamov selects his title. [P2-C01](source-map.md#p2-c01), P0002–P0033.


<a id="p2-c02"></a>
## P2-C02 — Public program

Gamov's two-hour broadcast announces the new currency, production incentives, terror orders, and ten-person Core. He promises safe streets by winter. Renewed weather attack ends the evening. The text gives no absolute calendar date. [P2-C02](source-map.md#p2-c02), P0021–P0066.


<a id="p2-c03"></a>
## P2-C03 — Courts, press, and the spy test

After more than a week of cyclone attack, the rulers develop the new shops, confession policy, courts, and rival newspapers. Karmanyuk's case exposes Mercy's limited power. A necklace prompts the Voityuk test: false northern plans conceal a proposed southern offensive. Elena's appointment is requested for the next day. [P2-C03](source-map.md#p2-c03), P0002–P0113.


<a id="p2-c04"></a>
## P2-C04 — Rival newspapers and court notices

The first *Herald of Terror and Mercy* and revived *Tribune* appear. Court summonses, death sentences, rewards, and appeal rights become public. Fagusta denounces the new government. Gamov refuses to arrest him and offers a narrowly worded denial about prior access to his article. [P2-C04](source-map.md#p2-c04), P0002–P0051.


<a id="p2-c05"></a>
## P2-C05 — Conference arrivals and the leak's consequences

Allied leaders seek aid. Elena makes her public government debut. Bar reports higher output, while Shtupa still lacks energy-water. One week after Woodworth planted the northern story, Vaksel appears and reinforces that flank. Semipalov warns that the deception may cost Zabon. [P2-C05](source-map.md#p2-c05), P0005–P0091.


<a id="p2-c06"></a>
## P2-C06 — Aid ends; Zabon comes under attack

Gamov ends allied subsidies. The enemy converts its northern concentration into an attack. Gamov rejects Semipalov's planned withdrawal and orders defense plus a central offensive. He then discloses the false-affair plan to Semipalov and Elena. Their performance is agreed before the proposed next-day inspection. [P2-C06](source-map.md#p2-c06), P0002–P0069.


<a id="p2-c07"></a>
## P2-C07 — The flood and Nordag's intervention

Semipalov inspects Zabon, detects Nordag's buildup, and prepares field weather defenses. Reinforcements need one to two weeks; the immediate flood lasts two nights and two days. Vaksel preserves much of his force by withdrawing from the expected impact zone. Nordag declares war after border flooding and helps encircle Zabon. [P2-C07](source-map.md#p2-c07), P0007–P0103.


<a id="p2-c08"></a>
## P2-C08 — Capture of Nordag's headquarters

Zabon's food lasts two weeks at normal rations, four at half. The government adopts the same reduction. A waterplane raid captures the enemy command; Putrament left two hours earlier. Semipalov feeds Voityuk an invented genocide threat. Gamov orders public coercion of the actual captives. [P2-C08](source-map.md#p2-c08), P0007–P0097.


<a id="p2-c09"></a>
## P2-C09 — Withdrawal and a death order

The officers, captured on Nordag's third war day, are displayed in Zabon. Putrament refuses their appeal at noon the next day, then announces withdrawal on the following day. Relief is observed. Gonzalez arrives with the officers' death sentence, to be published next day, and removes Semipalov's local authority. Execution has not yet been narrated. [P2-C09](source-map.md#p2-c09), P0017, P0033–P0038, P0069–P0113.


<a id="p3-c01"></a>
## P3-C01 — Autumn reckoning

Semipalov protests the reported executions but stays in government. Military recovery is incomplete; reserves decline and winter supply threatens. Gamov orders a campaign against bandits, while Semipalov prepares a credible information channel through Voityuk. [P3-C01](source-map.md#p3-c01), P0002–P0069.

<a id="p3-c02"></a>
## P3-C02 — The first-snow amnesty

After a reflection week and an amnesty week, Gamov announces 273,565 surrenders. The unexpectedly large total exposes poor intelligence and leads to a wider question: how to learn what the people think. Semipalov arranges an unnamed person's arrest. [P3-C02](source-map.md#p3-c02), P0003–P0032.

<a id="p3-c03"></a>
## P3-C03 — Voityuk is coerced

Semipalov arrests Anna, extracts Voityuk's cooperation, requests foreign funds, and releases her. His announced peace initiative had not existed before the interview; Gamov now takes it up. No bank withdrawal is yet shown. [P3-C03](source-map.md#p3-c03), P0038–P0132.

<a id="p3-c04"></a>
## P3-C04 — The Declaration and the Klur visitors

Gamov publishes the Declaration on War and invites peace terms. Foreign outrage exceeds the domestic response. Biber and Falk debate him in an audience that extends into its third hour. Gamov promises to face judgment after victory. [P3-C04](source-map.md#p3-c04), P0002–P0168.


<a id="p3-c05"></a>
## P3-C05 — Late winter preparations

Voityuk delivers the hundred account codes; successful withdrawals are not shown. Enemy preparations intensify. Semipalov visits the secret waterplane plant and sees why weather defense lacks energy-water. Elena asks about her delayed political role. [P3-C05](source-map.md#p3-c05), P0007–P0075.

<a id="p3-c06"></a>
## P3-C06 — Spring peace terms and a proposed vote

The enemy publishes peace terms. Gamov chooses an honest four-question referendum and promises to leave office if rejected. Ministers have worked together for almost half a year; referendum mechanics were prescribed two months earlier. These are different intervals. [P3-C06](source-map.md#p3-c06), P0002–P0059.

<a id="p3-c07"></a>
## P3-C07 — Mandate, invasion, assassination attempt, and Sorbas

The referendum rejects the enemy demands and government resignation. Vaksel attacks after the government's reply; new belligerents join a week into the offensive. Serbin saves Gamov from an attacker. Konduk bombs Sorbas in a separate event; preliminary casualties trigger a rescue effort. [P3-C07](source-map.md#p3-c07), P0032–P0093.

<a id="p3-c08"></a>
## P3-C08 — Konduk occupied

One hundred aircraft seize Konduk's leaders. Maravan-khor broadcasts surrender; ground troops occupy the country. Courts impose collective penalties. Recorded Torbash talks bracket the invasion by one day on each side, even though both recordings are narrated afterward. [P3-C08](source-map.md#p3-c08), P0010–P0099.


<a id="p3-c09"></a>
## P3-C09 — A frame is planned; the fleet mutinies

Attrition and floods continue. Pavel proposes the donation-and-abduction scheme against Bernoulli. Semipalov plants its supporting intelligence story with Voityuk. A report then confirms that aircrews have arrested commanders and threaten unauthorized action. [P3-C09](source-map.md#p3-c09), P0002–P0076.

<a id="p3-c10"></a>
## P3-C10 — The mutiny ends

The crews release their officers at Gamov's order. He pardons both groups, explains the need to preserve the full fleet's surprise, and creates four brigade commands for the mutiny leaders. The liaison's separate failure remains punishable. No precise new offensive date is set. [P3-C10](source-map.md#p3-c10), P0022–P0085.

<a id="p3-c11"></a>
## P3-C11 — Bernoulli is brought to Latania

A five-million-didan donation creates scandal but not yet the intended reserve diversion. Pavel abducts Bernoulli and delivers him on the third day. The prisoner refuses cooperation and foresees the false-agent campaign. Gamov still needs a stronger action; Semipalov conceals his new idea. [P3-C11](source-map.md#p3-c11), P0002–P0078.

<a id="p3-c12"></a>
## P3-C12 — False betrayal, real imprisonment

Semipalov proposes sacrificing his public identity. Arrest moves from next afternoon to next morning so it precedes Voityuk's escape plan. In confinement, Pustovoyt explains the safe apparatus. Elena visits the following morning, is denied the truth, and leaves after Semipalov's false confession. [P3-C12](source-map.md#p3-c12), P0002–P0158.

<a id="p3-c13"></a>
## P3-C13 — Noon at the gallows

Shtupa keeps Adan's sky clear for the execution display. Semipalov sees Peano's tears, enters the sack, loses faith in the plan, and is lifted into darkness. The chapter contains five source blocks and gives no external confirmation of death. [P3-C13](source-map.md#p3-c13), P0002–P0005.


<a id="p4-c01"></a>
## P4-C01 — Secret recovery

Semipalov awakens in a guarded house after a two-day bedside vigil. Pavel reports the ruse's effects and half of Floria lost. The former prisoner must remain hidden; he learns of two nearby scientists. [P4-C01](source-map.md#p4-c01), P0002–P0075.

<a id="p4-c02"></a>
## P4-C02 — The first laboratory visit

Shvurts and Kozyura explain their research and show a city and nuclear destruction through the screen. Semipalov witnesses images, not travel. The attacked city and its historical date remain unnamed. [P4-C02](source-map.md#p4-c02), P0003–P0113.

<a id="p4-c03"></a>
## P4-C03 — A week later, the concealment continues

Pavel arrives one week after contact. All Floria has fallen; Amentola's Klur conference is ten days ahead. Semipalov chooses to stay hidden until the speech. Kozyura's personal origin is qualified and the scientists' bodily changes are reported. [P4-C03](source-map.md#p4-c03), P0020–P0054.

<a id="p4-c04"></a>
## P4-C04 — Secret return and next-day orders

After Amentola's speech, Semipalov returns to the palace the next morning. He resumes command, leaves Elena uninformed, and orders the offensive for the following day. [P4-C04](source-map.md#p4-c04), P0002–P0040.

<a id="p4-c05"></a>
## P4-C05 — The fleet attacks

Weather attack and air landings capture Vaksel, strike the Fermor conference, and free camps. The prison-camp operation is the decisive aim. Amentola's fate remains unknown at the chapter boundary. [P4-C05](source-map.md#p4-c05), P0002–P0047.


<a id="p4-c06"></a>
## P4-C06 — The plan adapts and the deception is disclosed

On the second day, freed prisoners' weakness forces a supply-first revision. A later-narrated camp trial judges abusers and rewards Khota. Amentola is reported escaped. Within a week the Fermor raiders leave Klur. Gamov publicly reveals the false treason and staged hanging. [P4-C06](source-map.md#p4-c06), P0002–P0075.

<a id="p4-c07"></a>
## P4-C07 — Tronshke is encircled

Six centers shelter and arm the freed men. After a week's planned preparation, Latanian attacks join front and rear forces. Tronshke kills himself; some resistance lasts two more weeks. Less than a month after Fermor, troops reach the Roder–Klur border. The intervals overlap rather than necessarily add. [P4-C07](source-map.md#p4-c07), P0002–P0068.

<a id="p4-c08"></a>
## P4-C08 — Autumn leave and a deferred reunion

Weather warfare pauses. Freed prisoners go home on leave while enemy prisoners enter rear camps. Elena meets Semipalov; they explain their conflict, and he promises to return home later. The reunion is promised, not yet completed. [P4-C08](source-map.md#p4-c08), P0002–P0086.

<a id="p4-c09"></a>
## P4-C09 — Floria separates; Patina receives a constitution

The Core rejects wholesale deportation and approves punitive Floria independence and transfers. At Laina, Gamov confronts Patina's rivals, produces Torba, and imposes a shared Core, military administration, and a Women's Control Committee. [P4-C09](source-map.md#p4-c09), P0002–P0210.

<a id="p4-c10"></a>
## P4-C10 — Conditional relief and a new reprisal

The rulers plan outside aid to captives under heavy confiscation. New enemy raids destroy civilian settlements. Semipalov proposes an execution flight shielded by hostages; Gamov approves, and Peano prepares the base attack. [P4-C10](source-map.md#p4-c10), P0007–P0054.


<a id="p4-c11"></a>
## P4-C11 — Reprisal executed

Island-base attackers are captured and sentenced. The hostage convoy flies over Cortezia, drops condemned airmen, and returns. Civilians' nets save some but not all. Ten transports release the hostages in Klur. Gamov reads the welcome as a psychological turning point; Semipalov doubts it. [P4-C11](source-map.md#p4-c11), P0002–P0049.

<a id="p4-c12"></a>
## P4-C12 — Renewed opposition and delayed sentences

Enemy rearmament resumes. The conference trial sentences civilian war promoters. Amentola sends Torkin; Knurka's new White Court membership enables Shar's protest and a stay. Luiza and Zhanna's concealed identities are exposed to the rulers. [P4-C12](source-map.md#p4-c12), P0002–P0056.

<a id="p4-c13"></a>
## P4-C13 — Two failed private bargains

Shar asks protection for Anna and obtains a qualified remembered promise. Torkin offers Nordag's withdrawal for the conference captives, but Gamov rejects the bargain. Zhanna's execution is announced for next morning. [P4-C13](source-map.md#p4-c13), P0002–P0075.

<a id="p4-c14"></a>
## P4-C14 — Reported execution and maternal pressure

The narration reports Zhanna's dawn execution. Days later, Radon is reported to have killed Torkin. Gamov confronts the women delegates, keeps prisoner relief in place, and requests the Nordag invasion plan. [P4-C14](source-map.md#p4-c14), P0002–P0091.

<a id="p4-c15"></a>
## P4-C15 — Putrament surrenders; Semipalov is shot

A week of supply blockade drives Nordag toward surrender. Luiza faces a public ultimatum for noon on the first day of leaf-fall. Putrament arrives before her hanging and saves her. As Semipalov leaves, a woman fires and Pavel cries that he is dead. The result is not medically confirmed here. [P4-C15](source-map.md#p4-c15), P0002–P0136.


<a id="p5-c01"></a>
## P5-C01 — Survival and Anna's motive

Semipalov survives the shot and returns with Anna to Adan. She attacks the punishment of Floria; he confirms its intended effects and sends her to the conspiracy trial. Gamov reports Korina's epidemic. [P5-C01](source-map.md#p5-c01), P0002–P0071.

<a id="p5-c02"></a>
## P5-C02 — Home and the medical crisis

Elena and Semipalov reunite. She explains water allergy and the protective effect of natural milk. He resolves to choose home, then calls Gamov with rescue proposals for a Core meeting next morning. [P5-C02](source-map.md#p5-c02), P0004–P0074.

<a id="p5-c03"></a>
## P5-C03 — Rescue is adopted

Semipalov proposes dry weather and international milk supplies. Gamov assigns implementation despite Gonzalez's objections to helping enemies. Women's leadership roles are proposed. [P5-C03](source-map.md#p5-c03), P0009–P0043.

<a id="p5-c04"></a>
## P5-C04 — Implementation and release

The aid network develops. The conspiracy trial ends in release without acquittal. Konrad joins the guard; Anna accepts work collecting milk in Floria. Gamov later confirms the planned intervention with Gonzalez. [P5-C04](source-map.md#p5-c04), P0003–P0169.

<a id="p5-c05"></a>
## P5-C05 — Months of rescue; military action rejected

The dry summer and rescue save many but impose deaths and costs. After two days' deliberation, the Core votes six to four against renewed fighting. Semipalov accepts the decision without resigning. [P5-C05](source-map.md#p5-c05), P0002–P0034.

<a id="p5-c06"></a>
## P5-C06 — Famine, revolt, and a referendum call

Natural autumn follows the dry summer and epidemic suppression. Food shortages deepen toward winter. Pliss takes power after the Fermor revolt. The Core rejects foreign food aid seven to three; Gamov appeals to the people and refuses delay for treatment. [P5-C06](source-map.md#p5-c06), P0002–P0128.


<a id="p5-c07"></a>
## P5-C07 — Gamov collapses

At the assembly, Gamov suffers a heart attack. He orders conditional supply preparations before losing consciousness. Semipalov assumes acting functions, promises the vote, and arranges health bulletins; no aid has yet been authorized for dispatch. [P5-C07](source-map.md#p5-c07), P0002–P0041.

<a id="p5-c08"></a>
## P5-C08 — Seven-day preparations and sickroom broadcasts

The Core allows seven days despite Bar's longer logistical estimate. Putrament is released to organize Nordag's vote. Serbin's broadcasts turn the sickroom into a public political focus. Semipalov meets Gamov three days before voting and the women organizers two days before it. [P5-C08](source-map.md#p5-c08), P0002–P0195.

<a id="p5-c09"></a>
## P5-C09 — The vote and early dispatch

Eastern voting begins at 06:00 local time. Semipalov authorizes movement before the final national result, votes no at noon, then permits border crossing on Isiro's estimated eighty-percent yes. Reported convoy durations are not all securely confined to that morning. [P5-C09](source-map.md#p5-c09), P0019–P0047.

<a id="p5-c10"></a>
## P5-C10 — Aid arrives and alliances change

Klur receives the food; a short calm corridor enables the Nordag–Korina crossing. Agnessa makes peace with Latania. Pliss instead breaks with Cortezia and joins Latania's military side. Celebrations delay empty vehicles' return, and Semipalov admits his forecast was wrong. [P5-C10](source-map.md#p5-c10), P0018–P0092.

<a id="p5-c11"></a>
## P5-C11 — Cortezia declares peace

Terror attacks and a weak unofficial vote precede the first palace petition. The fiancé's death triggers a general strike. A second march reaches Amentola; Radon drops her weapon, and he announces the unanimous end of war. Amentola remains alive. Semipalov says “Victory”; Gamov says “Peace.” [P5-C11](source-map.md#p5-c11), P0012–P0133.


<a id="p6-c01"></a>
## P6-C01 — The announced dead return

At the Adan conference, named condemned people and hundreds of others appear alive. The ministers' secret coordination becomes visible. Real deaths are explicitly distinguished from this saved group. [P6-C01](source-map.md#p6-c01), P0004–P0043.

<a id="p6-c02"></a>
## P6-C02 — The system explained; world union proposed

Gamov explains the three-person secret and Semipalov's useful ignorance. Gonzalez acknowledges actual killings. The Core accepts Biber's world-union scheme for presentation to the conference. [P6-C02](source-map.md#p6-c02), P0006–P0061.

<a id="p6-c03"></a>
## P6-C03 — Union approved; trial demanded

The rulers approve the union and return to reorganize their countries. Gamov demands a public trial; earlier commitments with Gonzalez and the editors are revealed. Semipalov becomes the third defendant. [P6-C03](source-map.md#p6-c03), P0004–P0096.

<a id="p6-c04"></a>
## P6-C04 — A retrospective moral review

Semipalov reinterprets Gamov's earlier acts and discusses the proposed trial with Elena. Her reading of accountability challenges his fear of self-destruction. No verdict is reached. [P6-C04](source-map.md#p6-c04), P0004–P0050.

<a id="p6-c05"></a>
## P6-C05 — Months later, before trial

Government construction continues for months. Bar and Woodworth receive interim authority. Bernoulli, alive after imprisonment, warns against the trial and offers himself as a defense witness. [P6-C05](source-map.md#p6-c05), P0002–P0040.


<a id="p6-c06"></a>
## P6-C06 — Prosecution and the first authorship disclosure

After a procedural first day, Georgiu prosecutes government wrongs. A later exchange reveals Gamov's unsigned *Herald* authorship. The repeated adjournments create a numbering problem retained in C028. [P6-C06](source-map.md#p6-c06), P0006–P0046.

<a id="p6-c07"></a>
## P6-C07 — The second authorship disclosure

Fagusta reveals Gamov's authorship of the hostile *Tribune* editorials. The editors' ignorance of each other explains Fagusta's shock. The source calls the close the third trial day; it is not silently renumbered. [P6-C07](source-map.md#p6-c07), P0020–P0065.

<a id="p6-c08"></a>
## P6-C08 — Voityuk testifies

On a stated fourth day, the living Voityuk accuses Semipalov. Semipalov argues that good and harm cannot be cleanly separated. Gonzalez promises to rebut him tomorrow; Semipalov then goes home. [P6-C08](source-map.md#p6-c08), P0002–P0061.

<a id="p6-c09"></a>
## P6-C09 — Gonzalez confesses

The source again calls this the fourth day. Gonzalez explains his real killing, the secret Mercy system, and his demand for death. Biber argues from historical results. The ordinal conflict remains recorded. [P6-C09](source-map.md#p6-c09), P0002–P0026.

<a id="p6-c10"></a>
## P6-C10 — A contingency for the verdict

Peano, Kaplin, and Serbin offer force. Semipalov permits intervention only to prevent immediate execution and secure a worldwide appeal. The palace seizure is prepared, not yet executed. [P6-C10](source-map.md#p6-c10), P0002–P0041.


<a id="p6-c11"></a>
## P6-C11 — Individual guilt and unknown origin

Gamov demands responsibility for individual victims. Tarkhun challenges the caravan story and proposes another-world birth, but the court rejects proof by song. Semipalov calls the physicists after recognizing that Gamov still knows nothing of their work. [P6-C11](source-map.md#p6-c11), P0007–P0144.

<a id="p6-c12"></a>
## P6-C12 — Death sentence and intervention

The court sentences all three defendants. Semipalov signals the troops, places Gamov and Gonzalez in custody, and suspends enforcement for a worldwide vote. Preparations are announced for tomorrow. Gamov admits wanting a dramatic ending; guards prevent access to a weapon. [P6-C12](source-map.md#p6-c12), P0003–P0074.

<a id="p6-c13"></a>
## P6-C13 — Two weeks later, death rejected

The world vote overwhelmingly rejects the sentence. Gamov is released; the Black Court is abolished. Demobilization and factory conversion proceed. Gamov accepts Semipalov's proposed living departure to another world, but has not yet traveled. [P6-C13](source-map.md#p6-c13), P0002–P0088.

<a id="p6-c14"></a>
## P6-C14 — The passenger is changed

The Core and guards accompany Gamov to the laboratory. At the cabin entrance Gonzalez pushes him back, forces Semipalov inside, and activates transfer. Semipalov's knowledge of subsequent homeland events ends there. [P6-C14](source-map.md#p6-c14), P0002–P0049.

<a id="p6-c15"></a>
## P6-C15 — The memoir and the unfinished meeting

Semipalov awakens in an unnamed world's military center. Segovia interprets; Semipalov dictates and revises the account of several years. He discovers the hosts' technical-monopoly intent and resolves to resist military misuse. Guards escort him to a government meeting, which he opens. No negotiated outcome or homeland news follows. The final date/place are the author's colophon. [P6-C15](source-map.md#p6-c15), P0002–P0064.
