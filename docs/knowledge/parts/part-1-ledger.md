Official claims of advance conflict with retreats; Semipalov openly calls broadcasts lies (P0006–P0009). Compulsory volunteering and removal of workplace buses as “voluntary donations” expose coercive political language (P0019, P0022, P0033).
- **Continuity:** C002 partly resolved by P0017; no need to preserve the storm's military cause as wholly unknown. C003 is propaganda, not conflicting authorial history. Retrospective P0011 says both spouses later enter politics despite Elena's present denial; this is foreshadowing, not a contradiction.

Golden Wings is beyond the fifth; professional Latanian troops behind the third protect the Adan route. Leonid orders circular defense and reconnaissance. Semipalov requests a mobile sabotage detachment under his command (P0060–P0069).
- **Continuity:** C001's Patina warning is already documented in P1-C01 P0059, not an unknown prior event. C003 is now directly confirmed propaganda. C004 preserves distinct rumor, witnessed warmth, and Gamov's suspicion about Peano. No confirmed contradiction.

## P1-C05 — Raid and rewards

- **Coverage/consultation:** P0001–P0123 complete. Consulted all affected commander, front, weapons, money, and continuity records before adding Korkin, Komlin, reward rules, and C005.
- **Action/technology:** Inflatable suits and rafts enable the crossing; Pavel's secret Small Field Videoscope tracks the convoy (P0009–P0017). A three-group dawn ambush captures the money and 350 soldiers; enemy wounded also receive aid (P0024–P0032, P0073).
- **Numbers tied to events:** Gamov's tariff: 65 enemy dead × 200 kalons = 13,000; 350 captives × 1,000 = 350,000; 200 hand vibrators plus 30 pulsators × 5,000 = 1,150,000; two electro-guns × 100,000 = 200,000. Each wounded soldier gets 2,000 and each dead soldier's family 10,000. These last groups lack counts here, so do not infer a complete payout (P0042–P0044).

Gamov seeks motivation and political freedom through money and publicity; soldiers plan family support and threaten resistance to confiscation (P0021–P0023, P0050–P0093). Marshal Anton Komlin's dispatch, signed Western Front commander, admits its collapse but orders Steel Ram to stay. Leonid requests rescue permission; Semipalov prepares dismantling (P0100–P0123).
- **Continuity:** C003 publicity is purposeful. C005 explicitly records profitable combat versus the aim of making war repulsive; it is a debated doctrine, not authorial inconsistency. No confirmed contradiction.
