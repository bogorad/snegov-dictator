## P5-C02 — Elena, the old home, and water allergy

**Source coverage:** P0001–P0074, [original](../../../src/text/part0048.html), [map](../source-map.md#p5-c02). All paragraphs read. **Consulted:** P5-C01 (Anna, Floria, Korina); central Semipalov, Elena, Gamov, Gonzalez, Bar; places Adan, Zabon, Nordag; world weather generators and cyclone control.

- **People and relationships:** Semipalov assumes handing Anna to Gonzalez ensures execution; this is his expectation, not an execution witnessed here (P0002). Elena moved the old Zabon room into the Adan apartment after his hanging. Semipalov was an engineer and head of an automation laboratory (P0004). School friend Albert Loskin drowned trying to rescue a girl; Semipalov, who jumped with him, saved her but not Loskin (P0004). Do not confuse Loskin with Albert Peano. Elena keeps the former home free of Semipalov's official portraits; the narrator reads this as love for his former self (P0006). Their twelve difficult years of love describes the preserved home relationship, not a new explicit wedding date (P0006). They reconcile physically and emotionally (P0008–P0037).
- **Power and motivation:** Elena calls Gonzalez Gamov's devil but argues that Semipalov is an independent partner. Semipalov calls himself Gamov's trusted devil and later feels himself a hammer in unknown hands. These are competing self/relationship interpretations, not literal supernatural claims (P0043–P0051, P0069). He vows inwardly to choose domestic life, then immediately calls Gamov with two ideas; Core meets next morning (P0069–P0074).
- **Disease rules as Elena reports them:** Water vapor, not liquid drinking water, triggers coughing, vomiting, fainting, and suffocation. Longstanding sporadic water allergy in Korina has become epidemic; children fare worse (P0054). Cyclone floods allegedly strengthened a formerly weak virus, which persists after humidity falls; coastal Klur is suspect but has no established epidemic yet. Future spread to Klur, Nordag, and Latania is forecast (P0056–P0060). Stopping rain risks crop failure. Breastfeeding mothers and infants are protected; natural human milk can save dying patients but treatment takes weeks/months. Manufactured milk, used to raise about one third of humanity, lacks this effect (P0062–P0066). These are medical reports within the fiction, not independent real-world medical claims.
- **Continuity review:** Reconciles C01's general epidemic warning with the specific viral explanation and remedy. Klur is a newly named threatened neighbor in this ledger; geography beyond the stated coast is not inferred. The apparent hanging and return from death require earlier-part evidence. Semipalov's claim that Gonzalez must determine Anna's motive (P0039) suppresses what Anna has already told him in C01; treat as evasive speech, not missing knowledge. No confirmed authorial contradiction.

Dry weather for at least a year is initially proposed for the endangered coast; generators would move into Patina, Lamaria, and Nordag. Lyudmila Miloshevskaya mediates between Ponsiy Markvard, her party leader, and Vilkomir Torba in the new government; Gamov favors her ability. Luiza Putrament is proposed for Nordag (P0033–P0035). Radon and Norma are distinct people; this passage does not repeat Radon's name. Exact roles beyond these proposals await later evidence.
- **Reconciliation:** Woodworth addresses Gamov despite Semipalov's authorship and chairmanship; Gamov restores public credit to Semipalov (P0028–P0030). Elena praises her husband but he cannot promise another visit (P0037–P0043), confirming the C02 conflict between home and office. No authorial contradiction established. The dry-weather period is a proposal, not yet a measured outcome.

## P5-C04 — Aid implementation, the Black Court, and Anna's appointment

**Source coverage:** P0001–P0169, [original](../../../src/text/part0050.html), [map](../source-map.md#p5-c04). All paragraphs read; C05 begins later in the same HTML and is excluded here. **Consulted:** P5-C01–C03 (Anna, disease, women's committee, Semipalov/Gamov); central Knurka IX, Leonid/Pavel Prishchepa, Marutzian, Amentola, Gonzalez, Pustovoyt; world public claims and political mobilization.

- **Institutions and retrospective outcome:** Foreign governments initially did not join the Terror/Mercy joint-stock companies; Knurka IX wanted a White Court seat. Radon Torkin became a heroine after killing her husband; assassinations against Gonzalez's listed officials followed, while enemy police delayed promised rewards. Konstantin Fagusta calls this murder on credit (P0003–P0005). He is a journalist and former Optimates leader, opposed politically to the Maximalist defendants (P0031). Georgiu is mentioned as his journalistic opposite (P0006). These prior developments need Parts 1–4 alignment.
- **Political control and women:** Norma Friz supports Gamov publicly after his rebuke of the aid delegation. Lyudmila Miloshevskaya becomes known as “Mama Lyuda,” mediator above Markvard and Torba. Actual Patina power remains with General Leonid Prishchepa and Latanian occupation forces; the civilian spectacle obscures it (P0006–P0008). Retrospectively, Semipalov allowed public broadcasts of Luiza's accusations, freed her when Franz Putrament appeared, spared him, and sent both to Adan (P0009).
- **Rescue execution:** Norma organizes collection/preservation/routes; Amentola permits money transfers; Korina's queen accepts enemy help; Klur eventually agrees not to oppose weather operations. Shtupa moves generators west, Pustovoyt prepares foreign gold payments, Elena forms medical detachments for Patina and Roder (P0010–P0018). Milk-donor payments exceed senior officials' salaries in Semipalov's comparison; Fagusta satirizes the benefit imbalance with ordinary dairy farmers (P0027–P0028).
- **Trial evidence:** Marutzian, Marshal Anton Komlin, former ministers/associates, Anna, and Captain Konrad Komlin face the Black Court. Konrad is Anton's nephew, a former guard captain, and sole surviving member of the three attackers; two were killed by the crowd. Serbin appears among witnesses (P0029–P0042). Fagusta declined to prosecute and defends former political enemies. No Mercy representative attends, although the ministry is empowered to contest Black Court decisions (P0031). Konrad admits the attack but denies instructions by uncle or president; he cites loss of position/property/pensions, compulsory self-accusation forms, and terror's humiliations as motives (P0032–P0041). Other leaders deny guilt; Anna admits her act but denies criminality (P0032–P0033).
- **Legal result and design:** Fagusta disputes proof and asks what executing powerless former leaders achieves for the future. Gamov argues that their prestige made hostile speech causally dangerous, threatens Fagusta if his speech incites an attack, then promises no defendant will be executed (P0049–P0093). Gonzalez releases all, explicitly **not acquits** them. Konrad, now uncertain rather than repentant beyond qualification, joins Gamov's guard; Anna remains defiant (P0095–P0109). Gamov confirms prior planning with Gonzalez but denies briefing Fagusta; he predicted Fagusta's response and excluded Semipalov because his deputy would argue against the performance (P0122–P0135).
- **Changed relationships:** Semipalov's confidence in Gamov's ultimate purpose weakens: benevolent statesman or actor seeking glory is now his unresolved question (P0137). He nevertheless offers Anna rescue-committee responsibility under Elena, collecting Floria's milk. She accepts gratefully and promises large contributions. He repeats Gamov's appeal that a former attacker should someday rejoice at failure (P0154–P0168). Her change in attitude does not yet prove acceptance of Floria's punishment.
- **Continuity review:** C01–C02 expected Anna's execution; C04 resolves this by intervention, not by contradiction. C03 P0004 reported several Klur cases, while C04 P0012 says the epidemic had not entered Klur: retain as a **possible terminology/chronology tension**, plausibly sporadic cases versus epidemic spread or retrospective sequence; no confirmed error. Women's committee now includes Elena, Lyudmila, and Luiza (P0156), advancing C03's proposals. Gamov's plan is confirmed by his later explanation; Fagusta's collusion is explicitly denied, so Semipalov's initial inference must not become fact.

**Cross-part claim tension:** Independent Astra Part 4 review gives P4-C13 P0026–P0027: Shar proposes remembering that he requested mercy for Anna and Semipalov promised it; Semipalov accepts that record. Here P5-C04 P0149–P0151, Semipalov tells Anna that he answered Shar that asking him for mercy was useless. Preserve the differing retellings. This may be evasion or self-presentation by Semipalov, rather than an authorial error; a neutral statement that he consistently refused mercy would be unsupported.

## P5-C05 — Rescue costs and a six-to-four vote

**Source coverage:** P0001–P0034, [original chapter anchor](../../../src/text/part0050.html#1FLS9E-e57e33ea454c44b1aa8a6263a5c5ad60), [map](../source-map.md#p5-c05). All paragraphs read. **Consulted:** P5-C03–C04 rescue plan, Klur sequence, committees, political tension; central places Roder, Lamaria, Patina, Nordag, Torbash, Great Lepin; characters Bar, Pavel, Peano, Gamov, Pustovoyt, Amentola, Shtupa.

- **Time and observed outcome:** Several months elapse; military fighting stops, but no peace treaty exists. Latania avoids the epidemic; Nordag loses children and occupation soldiers, Klur's coast is devastated/evacuated, Roder imposes quarantine, and Patina has fatalities. No rain falls from Adan to the ocean throughout the summer. Gamov visits Roder/Nordag hospitals and refuses to reduce weather operations (P0002–P0003). C04's jubilant forecast that all children were saved is therefore **overconfidence contradicted by later events**, not a contradiction between two narrator reports of achieved results.
- **Economic reports, not all independently demonstrated:** Bar says energy-water reserves are exhausted, the harvest is poor, Marutzian's wealth is spent, Mercy's resources are nearly gone, and the gold lat risks becoming as weak as the old kalon. Milk pricing is a chief cost. Prisoner exchanges reduce camp-related income; only Torbash has joined Mercy. Gonzalez conceals Terror expenditures (P0008). Pustovoyt cites wartime birth-rate collapse and neutral mothers' reluctance to sell milk as supply limits (P0003).
- **Military assessment and geography:** Pavel says Cortezia now has the larger waterplane fleet and large energy-water stocks. Previous armies of Ferdinand Vaksel and Mart Tronshke are comparison points (P0010). Peano predicts a spring advance from Klur through Roder/Lamaria to Patina and a Cortezian expedition from Korina to Nordag; Latania lacks an ocean fleet. Amentola arms former Latanian allies. Great Lepin's Lon Chudin and his brother Kir Kirun are named threats. Peano claims an immediate Latanian attack can still exploit temporary superiority and surprise (P0012). These are forecasts, not completed campaigns.
- **Government division:** Pavel, Bar, and Peano privately enlist Semipalov (P0004–P0006). Their reports to Gamov had been separate. Gamov believes Amentola may share his wish to end fighting and that generals drive the rearmament (P0015). Predicted majority: Gamov with Pustovoyt, Gonzalez, Woodworth, Shtupa, and Isiro against Semipalov, Pavel, Bar, Peano (P0017–P0018). After two days, actual vote is six to four against renewed fighting (P0023–P0026). Gamov believes women's committees have effective international power and can stop war; Semipalov thinks states merely tolerate them (P0003, P0025). Neither prognosis is settled here.
- **Reconciliation:** Semipalov submits to the decision but refuses voluntary resignation; Gamov expresses dependence on him (P0026–P0034). A strategic split now follows C04's personal estrangement. C03's one-year dry-weather suggestion versus this chapter's completed dry summer is a plan/outcome distinction, not a contradictory duration. Klur's full epidemic now confirms spread beyond C03's isolated reports.

## P5-C06 — Food blockade, the Fermor revolt, and referendum terms

**Source coverage:** P0001–P0128, [original](../../../src/text/part0051.html), [map](../source-map.md#p5-c06). All paragraphs read. **Consulted:** P5-C03–C05 (epidemic, costs, women's groups, votes, enemy forecasts); central Amentola, Woodworth, Gamov, Shtupa, Pustovoyt, Gonzalez; places Cortezia, Roder, Klur/Korina local records.

- **Time and changed state:** Natural autumn follows the forced dry summer. Epidemic suppressed; women's rescue committees fade. **Radon Torkin**, here called Torkin's wife, has been acquitted by jury; her threats provoke no added presidential guard (P0002). Near winter, then before New Year, the chapter follows worsening shortages and revived protest (P0032, P0048–P0050). No calendar year given.
- **Food and claims:** Cortezia stops food exports to all, including Klur/Korina. It is a major industrial power whose chief export income nevertheless comes from farming (P0005). Amentola blames Latania's prisoner-aid deductions and weather operations; he reports allied harvests halved and Cortezia grain reduced by one third (P0007). Gamov proposes increasing prisoner rations to international standards as a response, rather than letting loss of parcels starve them (P0019–P0021). Bar's new exact report says **average** Latanian harvest, northern winds having brought sufficient water; Pavel's report says Klur **one quarter** of normal, Korina **about one third**, with nearly empty reserves (P0026–P0029). Keep these separate sources and denominators.
- **Diplomacy and demands:** Klur/Korina ration cuts, private-grain confiscation, and worldwide requests for purchases/loans precede Latania's peace query by stereo, used because diplomatic relations are absent. They reject separate peace. Amentola conditions talks on Latanian guilt, evacuation of Roder/Lamaria/Patina/Nordag, reparations, and removal of disliked leaders. Woodworth knew Amentola in their youth (P0032–P0044). Gamov's apparent depression is later interpreted by the narrator as developing physical illness triggered by political disappointment; that causal diagnosis is retrospective (P0045–P0047).
- **Fermor revolt and new ruler:** Norma Friz revives a Cortezian immediate-peace committee; Torkin's wife joins with her anti-Amentola movement. Klur's spontaneous women's movement demands peace/aid. A further **one-third ration cut** sparks palace and stereo-station occupation in capital **Fermor**. Regular guards decline to fight unarmed women. A boy of about eight appeals to “Grandfather Gamov.” Gendarmes remove women and boy; water cannon crush the revolt in a stated **ten minutes** (P0049–P0059). Corps commander **Arman Pliss** replaces the fled civilian government, bans protest, continues war, and announces restored Cortezian food exports. He previously favored shooting down Latanian planes even at the cost of their roughly hundred hostages (P0060–P0063; prior-part context pending).
- **Transport constraints:** Pavel confirms goods arriving at Cortezian ports and describes Pliss as honest/rigid. Shtupa says winter ocean storms prevent delivery; weather control could open routes only by consuming reserves for spring war (P0066–P0071). Restoration of export permission is therefore not proof of arrival.
- **Aid vote and motive:** Gamov proposes defeating hatred through food aid, while admitting the risk that fed enemies attack. Semipalov predicts Pliss will interpret aid as weakness; Bar says exports require ration cuts; Shtupa cannot assure the next crop under attack (P0076–P0100). **For:** Gamov, Pustovoyt, Gonzalez. **Against:** Semipalov, Woodworth, Peano, Bar, Shtupa, Pavel, Isiro. Total **3–7** (P0085–P0107). Gonzalez says his sins cannot be redeemed and he will face his own highest court; retain this as significant self-judgment, not a stated act yet (P0096).
- **Referendum:** Gamov appeals beyond Core. Semipalov dictates a question explicitly linking reduced home rations to feeding enemies who may defeat Latania; Gamov accepts it. He links staying in office to moral accord with his people and refuses treatment delay despite visible decline (P0110–P0128).
- **Continuity review:** C05 Bar's poor-harvest warning becomes an average-harvest exact report here: **revised information**, with possible descriptive tension retained. C05 says no rain from Adan to ocean; C06 attributes sufficient water to northern winds, without explicitly saying rainfall in that same strip. Do not invent a definitive weather contradiction. The committees' initial decline and later revival are successive events. Amentola's crop figures conflict with later intelligence by design; call disputed official reporting, not authorial error.
