# Characters and relationships

[Catalog index](index.md). Entries are added in reading order. Each claim names its source chapter. Later revelations must preserve the earlier evidence and distinguish aliases from separate people.

<a id="andrei-semipalov"></a>
## Andrei Semipalov

First-person narrator and witness at the Thursday gathering. He works in a laboratory; his exact profession is not stated in this chapter. He is initially skeptical of Gamov's warning, then recognizes that Gamov's speech has made him psychologically ready to follow. He fights several gang members and later reports Gamov's conduct with retrospective uncertainty. He first met Elena twelve years before this chapter; their marriage is strained but emotionally binding, and its duration is not stated. Source: [P1-C01](source-map.md#p1-c01).

<a id="alexei-gamov"></a>
## Alexei Gamov

Astrophysicist, philosopher by inclination, and physically explosive speaker. He shifts rapidly between anger, mockery, calm, and compassion; the narrator identifies these changes as a method used against opponents. He predicts that Marutzian's support for Patina will trigger world war the next day, calls the coming war purposeless and criminal, and argues that Cortezia is waiting for a pretext while Latania's leaders are foolish. In a street fight he uses extreme violence, then releases the injured attackers after ordering them to fear his name. He treats political words as material force. Source: [P1-C01](source-map.md#p1-c01).

<a id="pavel-prishchepa"></a>
## Pavel Prishchepa

Engineer in Semipalov's laboratory. He brings Semipalov to Bar's Thursday gathering and is a friend of Kazimir Shtupa. His father, General Leonid Prishchepa, has official contact with the meteorological service. Source: [P1-C01](source-map.md#p1-c01).

<a id="gottlieb-bar"></a>
## Gottlieb Bar

Host of the Thursday meetings, literature expert, ironist, and cynic. At this point he is chief engineer of a motor-building factory, but the narrator later recognizes a deeper nature as a state organizer. He enjoys presenting two convincing opposite opinions and values his role in the group. He argues that no large war can begin without visible preparations and that modern war is a meteorological contest. Source: [P1-C01](source-map.md#p1-c01).

<a id="john-woodworth"></a>
## John Woodworth

Cortezian who moved to Latania about ten years earlier and claims to have found his ideal homeland. He criticizes Latanian institutions while praising Cortezia, and supports party leader Arthur Marutzian during the Thursday dispute. He later helps Semipalov and Gamov against the gang and remains cool toward Gamov's theatrical threats. Source: [P1-C01](source-map.md#p1-c01).

<a id="arkady-gonzalez"></a>
## Arkady Gonzalez

Teacher of art history and specialist in ancient painting. His delicate, beautiful appearance contrasts with the narrator's retrospective description of him as a fanatic of cruelty and a strongman. The later description is reported knowledge, not an action witnessed in this chapter. Source: [P1-C01](source-map.md#p1-c01).

<a id="nikolai-pustovoyt"></a>
## Nikolai Pustovoyt

At the gathering, an accountant in a construction office with an oversized, asymmetrical appearance and a quiet, gentle voice. He questions why a border dispute should become war. The narrator later identifies his kindness and soft voice as genuine and names his future office as Minister of Mercy. Source: [P1-C01](source-map.md#p1-c01).

<a id="kazimir-shtupa"></a>
## Kazimir Shtupa

Military meteorologist and friend of Pavel Prishchepa. He reports planned climate stability, no dangerous weather disturbance, and an excellent forecast for the next two days. Source: [P1-C01](source-map.md#p1-c01).

<a id="elena"></a>
## Elena

Semipalov's wife. They first met twelve years before this chapter; the duration of their marriage is not stated. She has spent a month away at her father's home and returns despite believing that life with Semipalov is difficult, because life without him is impossible. Their conversation shows affection, jealousy, resentment, and fear of another confrontation. Source: [P1-C01](source-map.md#p1-c01).

<a id="arthur-marutzian"></a>
## Arthur Marutzian

Chairman of the Latanian government, defended by Woodworth. His promise to support Patina is the immediate trigger Gamov cites for war. A news broadcast reports that he has signed a decree to mobilize volunteers for Patina, ages eighteen to fifty-five. Source: [P1-C01](source-map.md#p1-c01).

<a id="amin-amentola"></a>
## Amin Amentola

President of Cortezia. The news broadcast reports an alarming speech by him after Lamaria requests help. Source: [P1-C01](source-map.md#p1-c01).

<a id="vilkomir-torba"></a>
## Vilkomir Torba

Patina's leader as described by Gamov. Gamov calls him a bombastic figure who may drag Latania into a fight for narrow interests. The chapter provides no independent confirmation of his office or motives. Source: [P1-C01](source-map.md#p1-c01).

<a id="leonid-prishchepa"></a>
## Leonid Prishchepa

General and father of Pavel Prishchepa. His official work brings him into contact with the military meteorological service; he favors the young scientist Shtupa. Source: [P1-C01](source-map.md#p1-c01).

<a id="bar-wife"></a>
## Bar's unnamed wife

An unnamed woman who serves tea at Bar's gathering and leaves. Semipalov says he never remembers her appearance, treating her repeated appearance and disappearance as her only narrative significance at this stage. Source: [P1-C01](source-map.md#p1-c01).

### P1-C02 state updates

- **Andrei Semipalov:** Receives a volunteer summons, is appointed captain, and is assigned as a radio saboteur in the Steel Ram division. His rank reflects a military-science course and field training completed three years earlier. He departs for Patina's wooded western mountains. Source: [P1-C02](source-map.md#p1-c02).
- **Elena:** Is mobilized to synthesize medicines at a pharmaceutical factory in Adan. She leaves the city the morning after her final evening with Semipalov. Source: [P1-C02](source-map.md#p1-c02).
- **Pavel Prishchepa:** Has formed two volunteer battalions and will lead a third. He recruits Semipalov into his father's Steel Ram division and explains its radio, resonance, and electro-artillery equipment. Source: [P1-C02](source-map.md#p1-c02).
- **Leonid Prishchepa:** Commands the Steel Ram division. Pavel says Leonid considers Gamov a born soldier. Source: [P1-C02](source-map.md#p1-c02).
- **Alexei Gamov:** Has become a major and deputy technical officer in Leonid's division. His military role confirms that his earlier violence and certainty were not only theatrical, while the chapter does not explain how he advanced so quickly. Source: [P1-C02](source-map.md#p1-c02).
- **Arthur Marutzian:** Delays ordering an invasion of Roder while speaking publicly in favor of the alliance. The chapter contrasts his cautious “volunteer” language with Amentola's open deployment of regular troops. Source: [P1-C02](source-map.md#p1-c02).
- **Amin Amentola:** Sends regular Cortezian soldiers and weapons into Roder and maintains an active intervention while Latanian leaders hesitate. Source: [P1-C02](source-map.md#p1-c02).

<a id="knurka-ix"></a>
## Knurka IX

Hereditary king of Torbash, a small state described as having no army or industry. He attends the allied conference in Adan himself. Semipalov's retrospective narration says that this apparently weak ruler later proves more intelligent than all allied leaders combined; this is a forward-looking narrator claim, not yet an action witnessed in the present timeline. Source: [P1-C02](source-map.md#p1-c02).

<a id="albert-peano"></a>
## Albert Peano

Twenty-two-year-old operator and planner in the Steel Ram division. He is Arthur Marutzian's nephew. Rumors say he is estranged from his uncle, but Semipalov has heard warm conversations between them. Peano says that people do not tell his uncle what he dislikes, and Gamov suspects Marutzian placed him in the front line to expose him to a fatal resonance strike. Peano is lively, technically capable, and able to plan fighting withdrawals. Source: [P1-C03](source-map.md#p1-c03).

### P1-C03 state updates

- **Andrei Semipalov:** Is now a major and commands a mobile sabotage detachment formed to strike into the enemy's encirclement. Source: [P1-C03](source-map.md#p1-c03).
- **Pavel Prishchepa:** Is now a captain and commander of divisional reconnaissance. He supplies a deliberately favorable report on Steel Ram at Marshal Komlin's request. Source: [P1-C03](source-map.md#p1-c03).
- **Leonid Prishchepa:** Suffers severe vibration after a nearby resonance shell explodes but refuses hospital care and keeps command. He orders circular defense and planned withdrawals. Source: [P1-C03](source-map.md#p1-c03).
- **Alexei Gamov:** Is promoted from major and deputy technical officer to colonel and deputy division commander three days before the chapter's main action. Leonid and Pavel now present him as a future army leader. Source: [P1-C03](source-map.md#p1-c03).
- **Arkady Gonzalez:** Serves as an operator who performs his orders but dislikes war and does not seek combat. He is physically powerful and technically reliable; the narrator calls his later role grave but does not yet disclose it. Source: [P1-C03](source-map.md#p1-c03).
- **Arthur Marutzian:** Remains at the center of the alliance's public story while his nephew serves at the exposed front. Gamov suspects deliberate family betrayal; this is not confirmed. Source: [P1-C03](source-map.md#p1-c03).
- **Vilkomir Torba:** Orders Patina to stop fighting and asks for a separate peace after Patina's defeat. He says Marutzian misled him by fielding volunteers rather than a professional army. Source: [P1-C03](source-map.md#p1-c03).

<a id="birker-shtok"></a>
## Birker Shtok

Roderian captain and deputy to Lieutenant Colonel Peter Parp. Captured after scouts destroy his vehicle, he resists death and torture threats but yields to Gamov's threat of filmed humiliation with a pig. His name is genuine despite Gamov's initial suspicion. He gives information while undrugged, then requests execution to atone for betraying his oath. Gamov keeps him alive. Source: [P1-C04](source-map.md#p1-c04), P0007–P0071.

<a id="peter-parp"></a>
## Peter Parp

Roderian lieutenant colonel commanding the guards regiment that escorts three hundred million captured kalons toward Roder. Shtok is his deputy. This initial information comes from Shtok's interrogation. Source: [P1-C04](source-map.md#p1-c04), P0055–P0059.

### P1-C04 state updates

- **Gamov and Leonid:** Leonid's injury prevents sustained command work. Gamov issues orders independently (P0003). His interrogation uses threatened humiliation, not physical torture; he orders a false heroic broadcast before the fighting occurs (P0026–P0045, P0079–P0084).
- **Gonzalez:** Eagerly assists the humiliation threat; his pleasure is observed (P0027, P0039). Semipalov's claim that he deliberately dirtied the pig is inference (P0040).
- **Semipalov and Pavel:** Will lead the night convoy raid with Gamov; Semipalov supplies the sabotage force and Pavel the scouts (P0074–P0078).

Source: [P1-C04](source-map.md#p1-c04).

<a id="filipp-korkin"></a>
## Filipp Korkin

General commanding Golden Wings. Leonid says he exaggerates his ability to resist; Komlin's later report confirms that Korkin's favorable messages conceal a division broken into isolated groups. Korkin requests urgent help as his last defense is threatened. Source: [P1-C05](source-map.md#p1-c05), P0006, P0100–P0106.

<a id="anton-komlin"></a>
## Anton Komlin

Marshal called Commander-in-Chief in P1-C03 P0004. His P1-C05 P0106 dispatch is signed commander of the Western Front. He orders Steel Ram to hold the Barta despite Golden Wings' collapse; Leonid requests permission to rescue it. The titles are recorded as supplied, with no demonstrated incompatibility. Source: [P1-C03](source-map.md#p1-c03), [P1-C05](source-map.md#p1-c05).

### P1-C05 state updates

Gamov distributes captured money as combat rewards without authorization. Pavel gives unconditional support. Semipalov objects, refuses endorsement, but will not openly oppose him; he explicitly says Pavel has the stronger claim to being Gamov's first follower (P0040–P0072). Leonid neither approves nor cancels the payments (P0097). Gamov explains that public fame protects commanders from superior interference (P0021–P0023). Source: [P1-C05](source-map.md#p1-c05).

<a id="semyon-serbin"></a>
## Semyon Serbin

Private in the second battalion. Leads demands for equal distribution of the captured cash and tries to force access to the vehicles. Gamov has him thrown into the latrine instead of executed, then orders washing and leaves open a chance to earn distinction in battle. Source: [P1-C06](source-map.md#p1-c06), P0022–P0054.

<a id="varella"></a>
## Varella

Soldier in the sabotage detachment. Challenges Serbin's lack of wounds while others in his section have been wounded, then helps subdue him. No first name is supplied. Source: [P1-C06](source-map.md#p1-c06), P0035–P0044.

### P1-C06 state updates

Semipalov prevents a volley into the mutinous crowd, proposes the westward rescue, and withdraws his objections to continued rewards for immediate practical reasons. Leonid approves the breakout despite Komlin's order. Gonzalez argues for rewarding kills over captures; Gamov rejects this, defending spared lives and induced surrender. By chapter end, the narrator describes himself, Peano, Gonzalez, and Pavel as Gamov's followers, without full knowledge of his plans. Source: [P1-C06](source-map.md#p1-c06), P0039, P0058–P0082, P0105.

### P1-C07 command changes

Leonid refuses to sign the continuing reward scheme; Gamov signs it (P0003–P0005). Peano designs the rescue deployment; Semipalov and Pavel lead blocking regiments, Gamov the heavy force, Leonid coordinates (P0015–P0016). After the rescue, Leonid makes Gamov corps commander, retains Steel Ram, takes the injured Korkin as deputy, and proposes Semipalov for Golden Wings. These appointments await higher approval (P0075–P0095). Peano's present reaction to the implied challenge to his uncle is unknown; later loyalty is retrospective (P0099). Source: [P1-C07](source-map.md#p1-c07).

<a id="danilo-mordasov"></a>
## Danilo Mordasov

Marutzian's emissary and Komlin's personal representative. Introduced as a general, he explicitly corrects the title to state councillor. He reports national losses and disorder, then demands recovery of the cash and soldiers' rewards. He reveals Marutzian's conditional order to arrest Peano. Gamov sentences him to death; Gonzalez kills him when he draws a resonator. Pavel records the meeting. Source: [P1-C08](source-map.md#p1-c08), P0017–P0100. His losses/corruption discussion is reported speech, not independent verification.

### P1-C08 state updates

Serbin earns rewards for two resonators, an officer, and a wound, then publicly reconciles with Gamov. The narrator's account of his later sinister influence is a future warning, not a present office (P0008–P0013). Peano openly accuses high officials' families of potential embezzlement; Mordasov quotes Marutzian's distrust and arrest instruction (P0072–P0077). Leonid declares complete support for Gamov after the killing and public statement (P0101–P0103). Source: [P1-C08](source-map.md#p1-c08).

<a id="sergeant-serov"></a>
## Sergeant Serov

Guard sergeant identified retrospectively as the man who nearly fired on Serbin during the cash mutiny. He joins the delegation promising to defend the commanders. No first name is supplied. Source: [P1-C09](source-map.md#p1-c09), P0006.

### P1-C09 identity and allegiance updates

- **Varella:** First name Grigory; Pavel's trusted scout and spokesman for the secretly organized delegation (P0008, P0020). Earlier entry's absent first name is now supplied.
- **Woodworth:** Now Marutzian's international-affairs adviser. Defects politically, preserves the corps' weapons and unit structure against orders, and urges Gamov to take supreme power (P0040, P0060–P0097).
- **Pavel, Peano, Gonzalez:** Secretly organized soldiers and isolated Mordasov's guards before the meeting, without informing Gamov, Semipalov, or the generals (P0018–P0032, P0072–P0073). Their initiative must not be attributed to a previously disclosed Gamov order.
- **Elena/Semipalov/Gamov:** Elena reunites with her husband and denies romantic interest in Woodworth. Gamov says he is unmarried and later asks about Semipalov's jealousy; romantic intent is denied and a later strategic purpose is foreshadowed (P0036–P0054, P0109–P0115).

Source: [P1-C09](source-map.md#p1-c09).

<a id="morokhov"></a>
## Colonel Morokhov

Head of government security. Reports the failure of city and then palace communications during the coup. His attempts to summon protection fail before Varella's soldiers occupy the chamber. No first name is supplied. Source: [P1-C10](source-map.md#p1-c10), P0053–P0084.

### P1-C10 final Part 1 states

- **Gamov:** Takes supreme power through a military coup and a coerced broadcast transfer. Described in the broadcast as a nonparty colonel; he accepts office but postpones the government list and program (P0118–P0119).
- **Semipalov:** Owns the coup's operational plan and orders removal of the former ministers. Gamov explicitly thanks him for the success (P0006–P0009, P0120–P0125).
- **Pavel/Woodworth:** Pavel coordinates captured sites; Woodworth supplies the prepared resignation and camera crew (P0039–P0044, P0106–P0116).
- **Peano/Marutzian:** Peano threatens to kill his uncle unless he accepts the transfer. Marutzian regrets sending him to the front instead of prison, confirming hostility; he yields and reads the statement (P0097–P0118).
- **Komlin/Korkin:** Korkin's demotion is now announced by Marutzian (P0051). Varella strips Komlin's insignia. Semipalov's impression of Komlin's mental collapse is explicitly disproved by his later knowledge (P0084–P0088, P0120–P0122).
- **Shtupa:** Reports to the government on enemy weather-station construction and harvest threats (P0028–P0030).

Source: [P1-C10](source-map.md#p1-c10).

## Part 2 — Offices and relationships in source order

### P2-C01 — From six coup leaders to a governing program

Gamov leads the six-person nucleus and chooses “Dictator.” Semipalov accepts humiliating punishments despite recognizing their criminal character. Marutzian remains under arrest; the former commanders' current offices follow the coup, not their earlier civilian jobs. [P2-C01](source-map.md#p2-c01), P0002–P0005, P0028–P0033.

<a id="anton-karmanyuk"></a>
### P2-C03 — Anton Karmanyuk

Police lieutenant colonel, about forty-five, father of three boys. He concealed bribes and a paid escape after reconfirmation in office; one escapee was his wife's brother. Gonzalez sentences him, Pustovoyt objects, and Semipalov approves hanging instead of drowning in filth. Family penalties accompany the sentence. [P2-C03](source-map.md#p2-c03), P0021–P0032.

<a id="pimen-georgiu"></a>
<a id="konstantin-fagusta"></a>
### P2-C03 — The two editors

Pimen Georgiu, former Maximalist close to Marutzian, receives the government *Herald of Terror and Mercy*. Optimate leader Konstantin Fagusta receives permission to revive the *Tribune* and promises fierce criticism. Their political hostility is explicit; Semipalov's future assessment of Fagusta does not yet disclose his final role. [P2-C03](source-map.md#p2-c03), P0034–P0047.

<a id="jean-voityuk"></a>
<a id="shirbai-shar"></a>
### P2-C03 — Jean Voityuk and Shirbai Shar

Voityuk is Woodworth's expert on southern neighbors. His Repentance Sheet omits his connection with Torbash envoy Shirbai Shar. Pavel calls Shar a Cortezian informant and links an emerald necklace worn by Voityuk's wife to Shar's family collection. This supports suspicion; Semipalov still considers a sexual rather than political payment. Gamov orders a false northern-offensive leak despite Semipalov's warning. [P2-C03](source-map.md#p2-c03), P0073–P0100. Voityuk/Voytyuk and Shar/Shirbay are English transliteration variants, not separate identities.

Elena is asked to become Bar's deputy. Gamov offers an administrative reason but withholds another purpose until Voityuk's exposure; Semipalov agrees to bring her next day. This extends [C008](continuity.md#c008), without establishing an affair. P0101–P0113.


<a id="eduard-konveizer"></a>
### P2-C04 — Public targets and critics

Eduard Konveizer, described as the richest banker and a military-corporation chief, ends the first list of one hundred summoned people, headed by Amentola. Fagusta attacks unlawful dictatorship, paid killing, inaccessible trials, and collective punishment. He nevertheless accepts one gang leader's degrading execution; he does not reject every use of Terror. Gamov protects him from the arrest demanded by Semipalov and Gonzalez. [P2-C04](source-map.md#p2-c04), P0011–P0046.

### P2-C05 — Allies and the enemy commander

Lon Chudin is president of Great Lepin; Kir Kirun is his brother. Knurka arrives with Shar, an ambassador for special missions. Elena appears publicly as a deputy minister. Omar Isiro is the quiet, competent information minister; Semipalov does not yet know why Gamov selected him. Ferdinand Vaksel is introduced as a four-star general and deputy to supreme commander Amentola. His arrival and northern troop movement are indirect evidence used against Voityuk. [P2-C05](source-map.md#p2-c05), P0005–P0030, P0071–P0091.


<a id="mgobo-mardoba"></a>
### P2-C06 — Alliance rupture and the staged triangle

Mgobo Mardoba, Sobrana's president, is about forty; Woodworth warns that his silent agreement can conceal opposition. Gamov suspends aid and rejects Semipalov's proposed sacrifice of Zabon. He assigns Voityuk to Semipalov and asks Elena to pretend to be his mistress, giving her husband a plausible personal motive for political rivalry. Semipalov agrees, but develops real emotional coldness during the performance. No actual affair is established. [P2-C06](source-map.md#p2-c06), P0002–P0034, P0048–P0069.


<a id="franz-putrament"></a>
### P2-C07 — Northern commanders

Franz Putrament, president of Nordag, declares war after its border districts flood. Semipalov is addressed as general and commands Zabon's defense; Pavel is now colonel. Vaksel, about fifty, is called marshal while still wearing four gold stars. Shtupa takes temporary command when Semipalov and Pavel leave for Adan. [P2-C07](source-map.md#p2-c07), P0010, P0025, P0040–P0042, P0098–P0103.


<a id="anna-kursai"></a>
### P2-C08 — Anna Kursai and the staged threat

Anna Kursai is Voityuk's wife. Semipalov still considers both an affair and an intelligence payment as explanations of the necklace. He knowingly invents a plan for mass extermination and deportation of Nordag's people, presenting himself as its helpless opponent. Gamov adopts specific coercive measures against captured officers, but this does not turn the whole invented genocide story into policy. [P2-C08](source-map.md#p2-c08), P0035, P0049–P0095. Kursai/Kursay is an English transliteration variation.


<a id="sumo-pariona"></a>
<a id="kinza-vardant"></a>
### P2-C09 — Captive officers and returning guards

Sumo Pariona introduces himself as colonel general and deputy commander-in-chief; Kinza Vardant is lieutenant general and chief of the main staff. Six other major generals complete the eight. Grigory Varella commands their guard; Ivan Serov and Semyon Serbin led the raid's entry. Semipalov interprets Varella's smile as mutual forgiveness after the earlier mutiny; the friendship is not independently proved. [P2-C09](source-map.md#p2-c09), P0006–P0017.

Putrament first refuses concessions, then orders a ceasefire and withdrawal. Semipalov rejoices, but Gonzalez brings an order to shoot the captive officers. Pustovoyt obtains only treatment of their bodies. The chapter ends with the sentence still pending, not a witnessed execution. P0033–P0038, P0069–P0113.


## Part 3 — Turning point

### P3-C01–P3-C04 — Complicity, coercion, and debate

- **P3-C01:** Semipalov threatens to resign over the reported prisoner executions, then accepts moral complicity after Gamov connects them to his threats. This is Gamov's political argument and Semipalov's response, not proof that Semipalov ordered the killings. Elena is in her second week of inspections. [Evidence](parts/part-3-ledger.md#p3-c01), P0002–P0023, P0067.
- **P3-C02:** Gamov values Fagusta's criticism but says it cannot measure the silent population. Semipalov asks Gonzalez to arrest an unnamed person; the next chapter identifies her. [Evidence](parts/part-3-ledger.md#p3-c02), P0023–P0032.
- **P3-C03:** Voityuk admits spying and names Semipalov as his source. Semipalov detains Anna without a stated offense, coerces her husband, then releases her. Voityuk says she knows nothing of the espionage. The secret audience of Gamov, Pavel, and Woodworth confirms that Semipalov's offer to replace Gamov is an operation. [Evidence](parts/part-3-ledger.md#p3-c03), P0023–P0067, P0108–P0128.
- **P3-C04:** Orest Biber, philosophy professor, and Arnold Falk, novelist, visit from neutral Klur. Falk glorifies war; Biber disputes Gamov's view of human nature. Semipalov reconstructs their interview from memory and guarantees its meaning, not verbatim dialogue. [Evidence](parts/part-3-ledger.md#p3-c04), P0025–P0033, P0046–P0052, P0143–P0163.


<a id="leonard-bernoulli"></a>
### P3-C05–P3-C08 — Opposition, loyalty, and religion

- **P3-C05:** Leonard Bernoulli is a former farmer, professor, leader of Cortezian independents, Amentola's enemy, and Woodworth's university contemporary. He opposes burdening Cortezia with Latania's former allies, not the war itself. Elena receives a technology award but complains that her promised larger role is delayed. [Evidence](parts/part-3-ledger.md#p3-c05), P0007–P0017, P0061–P0075.
- **P3-C06:** Gamov rejects Pavel's proposed ballot manipulation, promises to resign if defeated, and names Semipalov as successor. Semipalov says Gamov can be removed but cannot be replaced in their common work. [Evidence](parts/part-3-ledger.md#p3-c06), P0025–P0059.
- **P3-C07:** Fagusta explains that his criticism has accompanied support for the best available government. Amentola orders Vaksel to await the vote; Bernoulli urges immediate attack. Serbin saves Gamov from a knife and is wounded. Varella saves one attacker; Pavel suspects Komlin and arrests him, Marutzian, and associates. The plot's leadership is not yet proved. [Evidence](parts/part-3-ledger.md#p3-c07), P0002–P0017, P0022–P0031, P0055–P0069, P0089–P0092.
- **P3-C08:** Tarkhun-khor is Konduk's chief religious leader and Mamun's seventy-fourth embodiment. Maravan-khor controls parliament and ordered the Sorbas raid without a parliamentary vote. Gamov reveals that he grew up among Mamun's followers. Isiro heads the occupation commission; Kir Kirun is now explicitly a commander-in-chief. [Evidence](parts/part-3-ledger.md#p3-c08), P0004–P0007, P0028–P0046.


<a id="korney-kaplin"></a>
<a id="aircrew-leaders"></a>
### P3-C09–P3-C13 — Frames, mutiny, and a false execution

- **P3-C09:** Pavel and Semipalov plan to frame Bernoulli with Latania's own enemy-funded accounts.
