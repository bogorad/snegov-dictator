## Material source checks

- **Reward discrepancy, confirmed:** P4-C06-P0054 gives 2 dinas = 2,000 koras,
  20 drops per kora, and 1 gold lat per drop. These yield 40,000 lats. The
  same speech awards 2,000,000 lats in gold or notes. The current draft and
  ledger retain the narrated award and flag the factor-of-fifty mismatch.
  Catalog owner independently checked P2-C02-P0029–P0030 and reports that
  gold and convertible notes use the same lat unit. No correction required.
- **Marriage duration, cross-part limit:** P4-C07-P0024 says nearly twelve
  years married. The P1 first-meeting claim and all intervening chronology
  belong to the earlier-part review. Sent the exact later evidence to the
  catalog owner. That owner reports rounding makes the two claims compatible;
  no confirmed contradiction is established by this review.
- **Elena's words, compatible meanings:** P4-C07-P0026 reports regret for
  harshness. P4-C08-P0054–P0065 rejects repentance for hatred of the performed
  traitor. The ledger preserves this distinction without inventing an error.
- **Party attribution, confirmed local discrepancy:** P4-C10-P0002 makes
  Optimates say “your Ponsiy” to Maximalists, who reply “your Vilkomir.”
  P4-C09-P0195 explicitly assigns Markvard to Optimates and Torba to
  Maximalists. No change of party occurs. Sent to reader and catalog owner;
  C10 saved draft and ledger correctly preserve the known identities and
  flag the street exchange's attribution.
- **Roder occupation, unresolved scope:** P4-C07-P0067 says Roder is
  conquered and troops reach its Klur frontier. P4-C10-P0008–P0009 proposes
  full occupation of Roder and Klur. Conquest versus complete occupation
  could explain it, but the text does not define that distinction. Reader's
  saved ledger correctly keeps it as a scope tension.
- **C11 aircraft counts, confirmed text discrepancy:** P4-C11-P0024 starts
  24 aircraft, of which 10 carry prisoners; in the same paragraph 20 go
  toward the ocean, 10 passenger and 10 armed. P0044 returns 12 armed and
  10 passenger aircraft, totaling 22. No loss or detachment is stated.
  Also, nearly 200 conference hostages at P0014 differ from about 50 in
  each of 10 planes at P0023–P0024; the group at the meeting may be a subset.
  About 300 named captives at P0003 differ from 20 condemned in each of 10
  planes at P0023–P0024; a selected subset is possible. Sent exact evidence
  to reader and catalog owner; do not force all figures into one total.
  Readback: C11 saved draft and ledger now retain these distinctions and the
  aircraft discrepancy. No further correction required.
- **C15 name attribution discrepancy:** P4-C15-P0034 credits Pustovoyt for separating
  military surrender to the victor's will from civilian reliance on mercy.
  P0018 explicitly attributes that wording to President Putrament's appeal.
  Reader independently confirmed this. Saved C15 draft retains the directly
  introduced speaker; the ledger flags the later attribution. Readback passed.
- **Radon Torkin identity correction:** P4-C14-P0003 names the singer who
  shoots her husband; P0005 and P0010 repeat the identity. P5 draft C03,
  C06, and C09 incorrectly described her as unnamed or newly identified.
  Sent exact evidence to reader, catalog owner, and root. All three summary
  sections now use the established identity. Readback passed. Cross-part
  identity evidence is in the ledger. Final inline C03/C04/C06 ledger
  corrections were read back and passed. No identity correction remains.
- **Shar promise, inconsistent retelling:** P4-C13-P0026–P0027 records
  Semipalov accepting Shar's account that he promised leniency for Anna.
  P5-C04-P0151 tells Anna that he told Shar asking him for mercy was useless.
  This is a conflict between a shown conversation and a character's later
  version. Evasion or self-presentation is possible; it does not establish
  author error. Sent to reader and catalog owner. C04 ledger now records both
  versions and the uncertainty; readback passed.
- **Gonzalez's self-judgment:** P5-C06-P0096 says no good acts can redeem his
  sins and he will ask the highest court, himself, for punishment. This is
  now in both the ledger and C06 summary. Readback passed. It remains a stated
  intention, not a completed punishment.
- **C09 convoy time, unresolved alignment:** P5-C09-P0032 describes a day of
  travel and P0031 a border halt over half a day, before P0034 returns to
  Semipalov's noon vote. Summary and ledger now flag compressed/retrospective
  narration and preserve both durations without inventing a same-morning
  schedule. Readback passed.
- **Klur disease scope:** P5-C03-P0004 reports port cases; P5-C04-P0012 says
  the epidemic has not entered Klur. Detected cases and established spread
  can differ. The ledger retains the terminology/sequence tension without
  asserting a confirmed error. P5-C05-P0003 later reports affected coastal
  cities. This is not evidence that Latania itself suffered an epidemic.
- **Scientific continuity boundary:** P4-C02-P0070 describes narrowing the
  time focus of images, not a twenty-to-one relative time rate. No such rate
  appears elsewhere in Part 4. P4-C03-P0025–P0027 says Gamov had not yet been
  informed of the laboratory. Any later claim that these facts were stated
  earlier needs a retrospective-addition or attribution-gap note. Part 4's
  final ledger note correctly records this; Part 6 reviewer owns later proof.
- **Building-height scope:** Catalog owner reports P2-C07-P0011 places the
  Zabon headquarters laboratory on a ninth floor. Independently read
  P4-C02-P0083 says the cities' `дома` do not exceed five floors, then speaks
  of residents climbing. That noun may mean residential houses or buildings
  generally; a residential limit is not explicit. Catalog owner retains a
  qualified scope tension. This lane does not certify the earlier evidence.
